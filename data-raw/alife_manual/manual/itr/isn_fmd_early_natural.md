### isn\_fmd\_early\_natural

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Income  
<br>**Question**:  
Net farm management deposits or repayments: Early repayments - natural
disaster and drought  
<br>**Label**:  
Early repayments withdrawn from farm management deposit scheme due to
natural disasters and drought  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Total amount of early repayments withdrawn by the taxpayer which
qualifies for the natural disaster and drought assistance exemption.   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to eligible early repayments withdrawn from farm
management scheme due to natural disaster and drought. This amount is
deducted from total deductible deposits and is used to calculate farm
management deposit (FMD) tax concession. The taxpayer may be eligible
for the farm management deposit tax concession if they received a
distribution of income as a beneficiary of a trust that carried on a
business of primary production.    
The FMD holder may be eligible to access their deposits early, without
losing their concessional tax treatment, if they are eligible to claim
the natural disaster assistance exception. That is if:  
- they made the original deposit before a natural disaster declaration
was made  
- their primary production business received Natural Disaster Relief and
Recovery Arrangements (NDRRA) Category C assistance, and  
- they withdrew the FMD deposit early, after they received the NDRRA
Category C assistance.  
  
They may also be eligible for the drought exception. That is if:  
- for six consecutive months an area of their primary production
property has been affected by rainfall that is within the lowest 5% of
recorded rainfall for that area of their property  
- publicly available rainfall records held by the Bureau of Meteorology
confirm this low rainfall for the period of six consecutive months
preceding the month in which the repayment is made,   - and for that six
month period they held the deposit and were not involved solely in
primary production industries like fishing, pearling, tree felling or
tree transporting.  
  
Repayments of previously deducted deposits are included as assessable
income in the income year they are repaid. This does not include any
repayments that were reinvested, extended or transferred if they are the
caretaker of a deceased estate under certain conditions.    

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Add up early repayments withdrawn during the year that qualify for the
natural disaster or drought exception.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:

from farm management scheme due to natural disaster. This amount is
deducted from total deductible deposits and is used to calculate farm
management deposit (FMD) tax concession. The taxpayer may be eligible
for the farm management deposit tax concession if they received a
distribution of income as a beneficiary of a trust that carried on a
business of primary production.    
The FMD holder may be eligible to access their deposits early, without
losing their concessional tax treatment, if they are eligible to claim
the natural disaster assistance exception. That is if:  
- they made the original deposit before a natural disaster declaration
was made  
- their primary production business received Natural Disaster Relief and
Recovery Arrangements (NDRRA) Category C assistance, and  
- they withdrew the FMD deposit early, after they received the NDRRA
Category C assistance.  
  
Repayments of previously deducted deposits are included as assessable
income in the income year they are repaid. This does not include any
repayments that were reinvested, extended or transferred of if they are
the caretaker of a deceased estate under certain conditions.  
   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 2011-12, add up early repayments withdrawn during the financial year
that qualify for the natural disaster exception.
