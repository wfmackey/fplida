### pt\_is\_pship\_dist\_npp

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Income  
<br>**Question**:  
Partnerships and trusts: Non-primary production: Distribution from
partnerships less foreign income.   
<br>**Label**:  
Distribution from non-primary production partnerships less foreign
income.   
<br>**General Notes**:  
Total non-primary production partnership income or loss, excluding any
attributed foreign income or other foreign source income.   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to the share of non-primary production income or
loss from partnerships (excluding any attributed foreign income or other
foreign source income). If the taxpayer was a partner in a partnership,
they must also include salary, wages or allowances.  
This excludes, for example in tax form 2017-18:  
- attributed foreign income and any other foreign source income from a
partnership or trust (unless instructed otherwise); see questions 19 and
20  
- capital gain from a trust (unless instructed otherwise); see question
18  
- capital gain or capital loss in respect of your interest in a
partnership or a partnership asset; see question 18  
- income from a corporate limited partnership; see question 11  
- income from a public trading trust or a corporate unit trust; see
question 11  
- interest you received, or were credited with, from a joint account,
where the individual tax file number was quoted to the financial
institution; see question 10  
- that part of a distribution which relates to an amount of trust income
on which family trust distribution tax has been paid A5 or trustee
beneficiary non-disclosure tax has been paid  
- rent derived jointly (or in common) with another person from a jointly
held property where you were not a member of a partnership carrying on a
business of renting out properties; see question 21.  
   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Write the share of total income or loss from non-primary production
partnership.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1996-1997, this variable refers to the share of non-primary
production income or loss (excluding any foreign source income) from
partnerships.   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1996-97, write the share of total income or loss from non-primary
production partnership.

<br>**Event time line: Significance, Type of change(s), Description of
change(s)**:  

**1997**: Major; Introduced; New variable.  
Major; Introduced; Nonprimary production income split into nonprimary
production distribution from partnership
[pt\_is\_pship\_dist\_npp](#pt_is_pship_dist_npp) and nonprimary
production distribution from trusts \[pt\_is\_trust\_dist\_npp\].

**1998**: Minor; Wording; Question adds “(not including foreign sourse
income)”.

**2000**: Moderate; Component; Excludes any foreign income included at
question 15 or corporate limited partnership included at question 10.

**2001**: Moderate; Component; Includes income from partnership includes
salary, wage or allowance type income. Income excludes attributed
foreign income and any other foreign source income from a partnership or
trust, net capital gains from a trust, income from a corporate limited
partnership, income from a public trading trust or a corporate unit
trust, interest received, or were credited with, from a joint account
where the individual tax file number was quoted to the financial
institution, that part of a distribution on which family trust
distribution tax or ultimate beneficiary nondisclosure tax has been
paid.

**2008**: Moderate; Question; Excludes rent derived jointly (or in
common) with another person from a jointly held property where the
taxpayer was not a member of a partnership carrying on a business of
renting out properties.
