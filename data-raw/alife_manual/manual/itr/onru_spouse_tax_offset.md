### onru\_spouse\_tax\_offset

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Tax offsets  
<br>**Question**:  
Spouse (without dependent children or dependents).   
<br>**Label**:  
Spouse tax offset - used amount.   
<br>**General Notes**:  
Spouse tax offset amount used by the taxpayer.   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
Latest year: 2013-14. This variable refers to the spouse tax offset
amount used by the taxpayer. Spouse includes another person (whether of
the same sex or opposite sex) who was in a relationship with the
taxpayer that was registered under a prescribed state or territory law
or although not legally married to the taxpayer, lived with them on a
genuine domestic basis in a relationship as a couple.
