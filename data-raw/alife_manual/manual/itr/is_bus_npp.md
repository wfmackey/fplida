### is\_bus\_npp

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Income  
<br>**Question**:  
Net income or loss from business: Non-primary production  
<br>**Label**:  
Net income or loss from non-primary production business  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Net non-primary production income or loss amount from business
activities. Reported by non-primary producers that are required to
complete the business and professional items schedule.  
  
For income tax purposes " Primary Production " refers to a business of "
Farming “, and hence the term” Primary Producer “;” Non-Primary
Production " refers to a business that is not related to " Farming ".  
  
Primary production business activities include:   - cultivating or
propagating plants, fungi or their products or parts (including seeds,
spores, bulbs and similar things) in any physical environment  
- maintaining animals for the purpose of selling them or their bodily
produce, including natural increase  
- manufacturing dairy produce from raw material that you produced  
- fishing or pearling  
- tree farming or felling.  
  
The relevant indicators used by the ATO in determining whether an
activity is a business of primary production are outlined in Taxation
Ruling TR 97/11 - Income Tax: am I carrying on a business of primary
production?   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to income or loss derived from non-primary
production. This includes:  
- income as a sole trader  
- income under a pay as you go (PAYG) voluntary agreement  
- income from which an amount withheld because the Australian business
number (ABN) was not quoted  
- income you derived as a foreign resident from which an amount was
withheld because it was subject to foreign resident withholding  
- income of an independent contractor working under a labour hire
arrangement  
- income from specified payment such as payment for tutorial services
provided under the Indigenous Student Success Programme (formerly known
as the Indigenous Tutorial Assistance Scheme) of the Department of the
Prime Minister and Cabinet; payment for translation and interpretation
services provided for the Translating and Interpreting Service National
of the Department of Home Affairs; or income as a performing artist in a
promotional activity  
- any other business income  
- the small business income tax offset  
  
Excludes income from:  
- personal services income (for example, in tax form 2017-18, this is
recorded at A item 14: Personal services income (PSI) (is\_psi\_net))  
- foreign source business income (for example, in tax form 2017-18, this
is recorded at T item 20: Foreign source income
(is\_fsi\_other\_empl))  
- credit for amounts withheld from business as a result of the foreign
resident capital gains withholding rules (in tax form 2017-18, this is
recorded at H item 18: Capital gains (isn\_cg\_tot\_current).  
   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
The net income/loss from non-primary production is recorded under item
P8: Business income and expenses on page 11 as total business income net
total expenses taking into account reconciliation items and deferred
non-comercial business losses from previous year.  
  
Total business income includes: P8-D: Gross payments where Australian
business number not quoted; P8-B: Gross payments subject to foreign
resident withholding (excluding capital gains); P8-F: Gross payments -
voluntary agreement; P8-O: Gross payments - labour; P8-H: assessable
government industry payments; P8-J: Other business expenses.  
  
Total business expenses includes: Opening cost of sales, foreign
resident withholding expenses (excluding capital gains), contractor,
subcontractor and commission expenses, superannuation expenses, bad
debts, lease expenses, rent expenses, interest expenses within
Australian, interest expenses overseas, depreciation expenses, motor
vehicle expenses, repairs and maintenance, and all other expenses.  
  
Reconciliation items include: Section 40-880 deduction, business
deduction for project pool, landcare operations and business deduction
for decline in value of water facility, fencing asset and fodder storage
asset, income reconciliation adjustment, expense reconciliation
adjustments.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1990-91, this variable refers to any income from any business other
than those in primary production. This includes any payment received
under the prescribed payments system as a contractor.  
  
Primary production includes production from cultivating the land,
keeping and breeding animals or poultry to sell them or their produce,
fishing operation, forest operations, and manufacture of dairy produce
by the dairy farmer.    

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1990-91, the net income/loss from other business (not primary
production) is calculated as the gross other business income minus total
other business deductions.  
  
Gross other business income is the sum of sales of goods taken from
stock, gross prescribed payments income, and other gross business income
(from a profession, insurance money received for loss of trading stock
or profits, discounts, rebates, sundry credits, bad debts recovered,
bounties, subsidies and grants, and fees commissions, contracts,
cartage.  
  
Total other business deductions include salary and wages paid, payment
to contractors, depreciation of assets, interest paid on money borrowed
for business use, motor vehicle expenses, repairs, lease payments and
other business related expenses.

Calculation  
Business income and deductions for primary and non-primary production
are now recorded in the Business/professional declaration items Item 44
Business income and deductions on page 4.

Expense categories are recorded separately and include: External labour
costs (44E), cost of sales (44F), total interest expenses (44G),
depreciation expenses (44H), motor vehicle expenses (44I), repairs and
maintenances (44J), land degradation expenses (44K) and all other
expenses (44L). Employers are also entitled to a deduction for
contributions made to a superannuation, provident, benefit, or
retirement fund for the benefit of eligible employees or their
dependents. This is reported under all other expenses (44L).

Net income/loss from non-primary production business is calculated as
total business income less expenses add reconciliation adjustments
(44N). Reconciliation adjustments reconciles the business operating
profit or loss with the business taxable income.

**1993**: Minor; Component; Expenses categories added include:
Superannuation category (48G) and separate categories for lease (48J)
and rent expenses (48K) added under Item 48 Business income and
deductions on page 4.

**1994**: Minor; Component; Originally referred to as other business
(not primary production), now referred as non-primary production
business.

Income earned as an artist, composer, inventor, performer, production
associate, active sportsperson or writer may be entitled to income
averaging. Income must have been earned from an eligible activity which
includes advertising and endorsing products, participating in
interviews, serving as a commentator, prizes received for taking part in
eligible activities. The taxpayer must be an Australian resident and
their eligible taxable income must be at least $2500 in a year for the
scheme to apply.

Investment allowances is added to net income/loss from business under
item 47 Business income and deductions (47R). Investment allowances is
calculated as 10% of the capital cost of acquiring or constructing new
items of plant which costs $3,000 or more and quality for the general
investment allowance. This is added to net income/loss from business.

**1995**: Minor; Component; Also includes those who received income
under the reportable payment system.

Any taxable income earned as an artist, composer, inventor, performer,
production associate, active sportsperson or writer must also include
their eligible taxable income at Question 19 Other income - eligible
taxable income (see isn\_prof\_taxable\_inc). This amount will only be
taxed once.

**1996**: Minor; Component; Investment allowance discontinued from item
54 Business income and deductions.

**1997**: Minor; Component; Contractor, sub-contractor and commission
expenses added to item 54 Business Income and deductions on page 6.

Investment allowances added back in to item 54 Business income and
deductions.

**1998**: Moderate; Component; An activity is generally defined to be
business in nature if it:  
- has actually commenced  
- has a significant commercial purpose or character  
- is undertaken in a business-like manner  
- results in a profit  
- is carried out in a manner that is characteristic of the industry -
has repetition, regularity or continuity  
- cannot be more properly described as a hobby.  
  
Total business income from non-primary production is the sum of gross
PPS income (P6D), gross RPS income (P6F), assessable government industry
payments (P6H), and other business income (P6J) recorded under Item P6
Business income and expenses under 1998 Business and professional items
schedule.

Gross prescribed payments system (PPS) income is the total amount of PPS
income derived including any amounts of tax deducted. Contractors in
architectural services, building and construction, cleaning, engineering
services, motor vehicle repair and road transport may be involved in
PPS.

Gross reportable payment system (RPS) income is the total amount of RPS
income derived from primary and non-primary production. RPS applies to
certain payments made in the fishing, clothing, smash repair, and from 1
March 1997, fruit and vegetable industries.

Any assessable government industry payments, include any payments that
are assessable income received from government sources that has been
paid as a result of non-primary business activities such as bounties,
diesel fuel rebate, drought relief, employee subsidies, export incentive
grants, Medicare payments to medical practices.

Other business income includes gross sales of trading stock, goods taken
for own use from stock, gross earnings from services, bad debt
recovered, profit on sale of depreciable assets, royalties, insurance
recoveries, subsidies, employee contributions for fringe benefits, and
non-assessable government assistance from all sources.

Expenses Interest expenses split into interest expenses within Australia
(P6Q) and interest expenses earned overseas (P6R) under Item P6 Business
income and expenses under 1998 Business and professional items schedule.

Reconciliation items include drought investment allowances (P6U)
calculated as 10% of the cost of new property to fight the effects of
drought (limit of $5,000), environment protection or environmental
impact study expenses (P6V), landcare operations and water
conservation/conveying expenses (P6W) and other reconciliation
adjustments (P6X).

**2000**: Minor; Component; Other business income includes earnings as a
non-employee taxi driver.

Reconciliation item ‘other reconciliation adjustments’ split into income
reconciliation adjustments (P6X) and expense reconciliation adjustment
(P6H). Income reconciliation adjustments includes income add backs -
income not shown in the profit or loss statement but is assessable and
income subtractions - income shown in the profit or loss statement but
is not assessable income. Expense reconciliation adjustments include
expense add backs - expenses shown in the accounts which are not tax
deductible and expense subtractions - items shown as expenses which are
deductible for expense purposes.

**2001**: Major; Component; New categorisation of non-primary production
income reported under item P8 business income and expenses on page 10.
New categories include:  
- income under a Pay As You Go (PAYG) voluntary agreement (P8F)  
- income from which an amount was withheld because they did not quote
their Australian Business Number (ABN) (P8D)  
- income as an independent contractor under a labour hire arrangement or
income from a specified payment including payment for tutorial services
provided for the Aboriginal Tutorial Assistance Scheme of the Department
of Education, Training and Youth Affairs, and payment for translation
and interpretation services for the Translating and Interpreting Service
of the Department of Immigration and Multicultural Affairs (P8O)   If
they are self-employed, or driving a taxi under a standard bailment
agreement with an owner/operator.  
Excludes any income reported under Question 13 Net personal services
income (is\_psi\_net).  
  
Moderate; Component; Label P8O “Gross payments - labour hire or other

and included as a component of total non-primary production business

If the taxpayer derived income from business under the simplified tax

**2008**: Minor; Component; If the taxpayer derived income from a
