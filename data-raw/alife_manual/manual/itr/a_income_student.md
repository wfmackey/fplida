### a\_income\_student

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Adjustments  
<br>**Question**:  
Part-year tax-free threshold: Income while a full time student  
<br>**Label**:  
Income while full-time student for part-year tax-free threshold  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Amount of income earned by the taxpayer while being a full time student
less deductible expenses. Applicable if full-time education ceased for
the first time during the income year. Net income includes income earned
through salary and wages, non-salary and wage incomes, and income
received from their own business, partnerships, or beneficiary of a
trust.  
  
If you left full-time education for the first time in a particular
financial year, you can only claim the tax-free threshold for the months
you were not studying full-time. However, if you were working while you
were studying, you can add on your net income for these months to work
out your tax-free threshold.  
  
Note: The part-year tax free threshold for students that ceased fulltime
education in the income year was removed for the 2007 income year and
the label on the return form was discontinued.     

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
Latest year 2005-06: this variable refers to the net income earned while
being a full-time student if taxpayer left full-time education for the
first time during the year and they are able to claim the tax-free
threshold for the months they were not studying full time.  
  
Stopped studying full-time if  
- studied full time at a school, college, university or similar
institution at any time for 2005–06, and  
- were not studying full time on 30 June 2006, and  
- if full-time education stopped between 1 March 2006 and 30 June 2006,
and they did not begin another course of full-time education within four
months of when they stopped.  
   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
For latest year 2005-06: add all net income in the following three
categories:  
(1) salary or wages  
(2) non-salary and wage income – for example, dividends, interest and
rent, and  
(3) income received  
- from carrying on your own business  
- as a partner in a partnership  
- as a beneficiary of a trust.  
   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1990-91, this variable refers to the net income earned while being a
full-time student if taxpayer left full-time education for the first
time during the year and they are able to claim the tax-free threshold
for the months they were not studying full time.  
  
Taxpayer stopped full-time education if:  
- studied full time at a school, college, university or similar
institution at any time during the period 1 July 1990 to 29 June 1991,
and  
- were not studying full time on 30 June 1991, and  
- if full-time education stopped between 1 March 1991 and 30 June 1991,
and they did not begin another course of full-time education within four
months of stopping the earlier one.  
   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1990-91, add all net income in the following three categories:  
(1) salary or wages  
(2) non-salary and wage income – for example, dividends, interest and
rent, and  
(3) income received:  
- from carrying on your own business  
- as a partner in a partnership  
- as a beneficiary of a trust.
