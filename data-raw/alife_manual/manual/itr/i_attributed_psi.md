### i\_attributed\_psi

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Income  
<br>**Question**:  
Attributed personal services income  
<br>**Label**:  
Attributed personal services income  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Income attributed to an individual in exchange for personal efforts or
skills.  

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to personal services income attributed to the
taxpayer from a company, partnership or trust. If the payment is related
to the services done as a sole trader, this is reported under P1:
Personal Services Income (PSI) in the Business and professional items
section (see is\_psi\_net).  
  
Personal services income is income that is mainly a reward for your
personal efforts or skills and is generally paid either to you or to a
personal services entity (a company, partnership or trust).  
  
If your personal services income is paid to a personal services entity,
the income (less certain deductions relating to gaining or producing
that income) is attributed to you unless:  
- the personal services entity gained the income in the course of
conducting a personal services business, or  
- the income was promptly paid to you by the entity as salary.  
   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Calculate the total amount of personal services income attributed.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 2000-01, this variable refers to personal services income attributed
to the taxpayer from a company, partnership or trust. If the payment is
related to the services done as a sole trader, this is reported under
P1: Personal Services Income (PSI) in the Business and professional
items section (see is\_psi\_net).
