### d\_div

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Deductions  
<br>**Question**:  
Dividend deductions  
<br>**Label**:  
Deductible amount of expenses incurred in earning dividends  
<br>**General Notes**:  
This variable reports deduction for expenses paid on dividends and other
dividend related costs.  
  
From the 2009-10 individual return form onwards, the interest deduction
amount claimed by the taxpayer is listed as a separate component from
the dividend deduction amount.  
There were 278 instances of taxpayers that lodged a 2009 tax return form
for the 2010 income year and reported a value corresponding to the
combined dividends and interest amount contained in extinct variable
intst\_and\_div\_ddctns\_amt and these values were manually assigned to
the variable d\_div and d\_interest based upon longitudinal analysis of
the relevant taxpayers’.  

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
Taxpayers are able to claim a deduction on expenses incurred on
dividends.   
Expenses include:  
- fees for investment advice relating to changes in the mix of
investments  
- interest paid on money borrowed to purchase shares or similar
investments  
- costs relating to managing your investments, such as travel and buying
specialist investment journals or subscriptions  
- 50% of a ‘listed investment company’ (LIC) capital gain amount if
taxpayer was an Australian resident when they received the LIC capital
gain amount.  
- taxpayer’s share of joint share investments, or similar investments  
- interest expense from borrowed money to purchase assets for
income-producing investments  
- proportion of the decline in value of computer used to manage
investment.  
  
Excludes any expenses incurred in earning trust and partnership
distributions or an expenses incurred in earning foreign source
dividends.  
   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Total of all deductible dividend expenses.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 2009-10, taxpayers are able to claim a deduction on expenses
incurred on dividends.   Expenses include:  
- fees for investment advice relating to changes in the mix of
investments  
- interest paid on money borrowed to purchase shares or similar
investments  
- costs relating to managing your investments, such as travel and buying
specialist investment journals or subscriptions.  
- 50% of a ‘listed investment company’ (LIC) capital gain amount if
taxpayer was an Australian resident when they received the LIC capital
gain amount.   - taxpayer’s share of joint share investments, or similar
investments  
- interest expense from borrowed money to purchase assets for
income-producing investments  
- proportion of the decline in value of computer used to manage
investment.  
Excludes any expenses incurred in earning trust and partnership
distributions or an expenses incurred in earning foreign source
dividends.  
   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 2009-10, total of all deductible dividend expenses.
