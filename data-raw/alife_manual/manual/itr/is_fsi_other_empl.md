### is\_fsi\_other\_empl

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Income  
<br>**Question**:  
Foreign source income and foreign assets or property: Other net foreign
employment income  
<br>**Label**:  
Income from foreign employment not shown on a PAYG payment summary -
foreign employment  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Foreign source employment income not shown on a PAYG payment summary -
foreign employment less deductible expenses.  

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to income earned working overseas as an employee,
such as salary, wages, commissions, bonuses or allowance. This does not
include foreign employment as shown on a PAYG payment summary - foreign
employment. Foreign employment income is exempt from Australian tax if
there are privileges and immunities agreement or a law covering persons
connected with international organisations, or specific exemptions for
the pay and allowances of members of the Australian Defence Force
related to qualifying service in a declared operational area.   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
(a) Add up all gross foreign employment income amounts (this does not
include foreign employment as shown on a PAYG payment summary - foreign
employment) before any foreign tax was taken out.   (b) Add up all
deductible expenses incurred in earning the foreign employment income
(do not take away debt deductions).  
(c) Take (b) away from (a) and transfer to is\_fsi\_other\_empl.  
   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 2009-10, this variable refers to income earned working overseas as
an employee, such as salary, wages, commissions, bonuses or allowance.
This does not include foreign employment as shown on a PAYG payment
summary - foreign employment. Foreign employment income is exempt from
Australian tax if there are privileges and immunities agreement or a law
covering persons connected with international organisations, or specific
exemptions for the pay and allowances of members of the Australian
Defence Force related to qualifying service in a declared operational
area.  

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 2009-10,   (a) Add up all gross foreign employment income amounts
(this does not include foreign employment as shown on a PAYG payment
summary - foreign employment) before any foreign tax was taken out.  
(b) Add up all deductible expenses incurred in earning the foreign
employment income (do not take away debt deductions) (c) Take (b) away
from (a) and transfer to is\_fsi\_other\_empl.
