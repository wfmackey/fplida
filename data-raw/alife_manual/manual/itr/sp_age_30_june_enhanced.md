<br>**Label**:  
DERIVED: Spouse age with cleaning, interpolation, and enhancement  
<br>**General Notes**:  
The variable captures the age of the taxpayer’s spouse (in years) as at
30th of June of the income year. Prior to the 2013 tax return form, only
a subset of the lodging population were required to report details for
their spouse.  
  
Spouse’s age is captured primarily from the tax return form. Information
has been cleaned and interpolated to fill gaps where spouse age
information was not provided or where inconsistencies were apparent.
