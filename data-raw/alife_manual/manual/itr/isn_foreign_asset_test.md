### isn\_foreign\_asset\_test

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Income  
<br>**Question**:  
Foreign source income and foreign assets or property: During the year
did you own, or have an interest in assets located outside Australia,
which had a total value of AUD$50,000 or more?  
<br>**Label**:  
Indicator of interest in assets located outside Australia with total
value of AUD$50,000 or more  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Binary indicator of whether the taxpayer held any interest in assets
located outside Australia with total value of AUD$50,000 or more.  

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable is a binary indicator of whether the taxpayer had interest
in assets located outside Australia with total value of AUD$50,000 or
more during the income year. Assets include real estate, shares in
companies and other entities, interests in partnerships or trusts,
businesses, debentures, bonds, money and funds held in accounts or by
other parties, loans to other parties and deposits. They also include
intangible property such as trademarks, copyrights, patents, debtors or
‘equitable choses in action’. Assets also include any interest, whether
legal or beneficial, and whether held directly or indirectly through one
or more interposed entities. This excludes any assets held overseas
covered under question 19 Foreign entities (is\_cfc).    

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Print X in the Yes box if the value of overseas assets was AUD$50,000 or
more. Otherwise print X in the No box.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1999-2000, this variable is a binary indicator of whether the
taxpayer had interest in assets located outside Australia with total
value of AUD$50,000 or more during the income year. Assets include real
estate, shares in companies and other entities, interests in
partnerships or trusts, businesses, debentures, bonds, money and funds
held in accounts or by other parties, loans to other parties and
deposits. They also include intangible property such as trademarks,
copyrights, patents, debtors or ‘equitable choses in action’. Assets
also include any interest, whether legal or beneficial, and whether held
directly or indirectly through one or more interposed entities.   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1999-2000, print X in the Yes box if the value of overseas assets
was AUD$50,000 or more. Otherwise print X in the No box.
