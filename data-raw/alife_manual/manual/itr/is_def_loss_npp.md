### is\_def\_loss\_npp

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Income  
<br>**Question**:  
Deferred non-commercial business losses: Non-primary production deferred
losses  
<br>**Label**:  
Non-primary production deferred losses  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Total amount of eligible non-primary production deferred losses from
partnership and sole trader business activities.  

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to deferred non-commercial non-primary production
business losses if during the income year, the taxpayer either conducted
a non-primary production business activity as a partner in a partnership
or as a sole trader which resulted in a loss or resulted in a loss after
deducting expenses.  
  
There are special rules relating to the deferral of non-commercial
business losses. This applies to both Australian and foreign business
activities. Any loss during the income year from business activities as
a sole trader or partnership can be used to calculate the current
taxable income if:  
- an exception applies  
- taxpayer meets the income requirement and one of the four tests is
satisfied, or  
- the Commissioner has exercised his discretion or ruled that it will be
exercised to allow a claim on loss.    
If the taxpayer is unable to claim a loss during this income year, they
may defer it to future income years and this deferred loss is treated as
a deduction when calculating any net profit or loss from the activity in
that future year.  
   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Did you:  
- make a net loss from a business activity as a partner in a partnership
or sole trader, and   - did your net loss from that business activity
not come within an exception described at question P9: Business and
professional items, and   - did not meet the income requirement and the
net loss from that business activity satisfied one of the four tests,
and  
- have not been advised in writing that the Commissioner will exercise
his discretion to allow a claim of net loss from that business activity
for the year.  
  
If yes, calculate the sum of non-primary production portion of total
deferred losses from total deferred loss reported under a business
activity as a partner in a partnership (isn\_def\_loss\_partnership) and
as a sole trader (isn\_def\_sole\_trader) and record it under this
variable

<br>**Event time line: Significance, Type of change(s), Description of
change(s)**:  

**2006**: Major; Split / Merge; Variable is\_def\_loss\_pp\_npp
discontinued in 2006 due to separate reporting of of deferred losses for
primary and non-primary producers.

**2007**: Major; Introduced; New variable.

**2010**: Major; Component; The Tax Laws Amendment (Small Business and
General Business Tax Break) Act 2009 was introduced during 2009-10,
which allows an investment tax break for Australian businesses.

Therefore, a taxpayer can use the 2009-10 loss from a business conducted
as a partner in a partnership or sole trader to calculate current year
taxable income if the loss is due solely to a deduction claimed under
the small business and general business tax break.

As well as meeting the four tests to be able to use the 2009-10 loss to
calculate current year taxable income, the taxpayer must also meet
additional income requirements as outlined in P9 Business loss activity
details in Business and professional items 2010
(<https://www.ato.gov.au/Forms/Business-and-professional-items-2009-10/>).

**2013**: Major; Component; Removal of the small business and general
business tax break exception for non-commercial business losses.
