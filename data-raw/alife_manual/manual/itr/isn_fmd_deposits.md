### isn\_fmd\_deposits

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Income  
<br>**Question**:  
Net farm management deposits or repayments: Deductible deposits  
<br>**Label**:  
Total deductible farm management deposits  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Total amount of deductible deposits made by the taxpayer in the
respective income year.  

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to the total farm management deductible deposits
made during the income year. This amount is used to calculate farm
management deposit tax concession. The taxpayer may be eligible for the
farm management deposit (FMD) tax concession if they received a
distribution of income as a beneficiary of a trust that carried on a
business of primary production.    
Farm management deposits are deductible if, during the income year, the
taxpayer (FMD owner) satisfied the following:  
- taxable non-primary production income is under a threshold amount (for
example, in 2017-18: it is $100,000 or less)   
- was carrying on a primary production business at the time of making
the deposit  
- if they stopped carrying on a primary production business during the
year, they recommenced carrying on such a business within 120 days  
- individual deposits were not less than a given individual limit and
total deposit was not more than a given account limit (for example, in
2017-18: individual limit is $1,000 and account limit is $800,000)  
- individual deposits did not cause your total FMDs to exceed the
account limit  
- individual deposits did not exceed the amount of taxable primary
production income in that year  
- they held the deposits for at least 12 months or qualify for the early
repayment exceptions.  
  
Some deposits cannot be deducted if they were made in the year and
during the year the FMD owner:  
- became bankrupt or ceased to carry on a primary production business
(including a business that was carried on by a partnership or by a
trust) for 120 days or more, or  
- died.  
Deposits that were paid within 12 months are also non-deductible.      

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Add up deductible deposits made during the income year.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 2011-12, this variable refers to the total farm management deductive
deposits made during the income year. This amount is used to calculate
farm management deposit tax concession. The taxpayer may be eligible for
the farm management deposit (FMD) tax concession if they received a
distribution of income as a beneficiary of a trust that carried on a
business of primary production.    
Farm management deposits are deductible if, during the income year, the
taxpayer (FMD owner)satisfied the following:  
- taxable non-primary production income is $65,000 or less    
- was carrying on a primary production business at the time of making
the deposit  
- if they stopped carrying on a primary production business during the
year, they recommenced carrying on such a business within 120 days  
- individual deposits were not less than $1,000 and total deposits not
more than $400,000  
- individual deposits did not cause your total FMDs to exceed the
$400,000 account limit  
- individual deposits did not exceed the amount of taxable primary
production income in that year  
- they held the deposits for at least 12 months or qualify for the early
repayment exceptions.  
  
Some deposits cannot be deducted if they were made in the year and
during the year the FMD owner:  
- became bankrupt or ceased to carry on a primary production business
(including a business that was carried on by a partnership or by a
trust) for 120 days or more, or  
- died.  
Deposits that were paid within 12 months are also non-deductible.      

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 2011-12, add up deductible deposits made during the income year.
