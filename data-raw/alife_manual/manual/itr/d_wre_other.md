### d\_wre\_other

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Deductions  
<br>**Question**:  
Other work related expenses  
<br>**Label**:  
Other work related deductions  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Other work-related expenses deductible amount.  

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
Taxpayer can claim other work related expenses (see Individual tax
return instruction for more details).  

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Total of all deductions for other work-related expenses.  

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1991-92, taxpayer can claim for other work-related expenses.
Excludes business related expenses.  

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1991-92, total of all deductions for other work-related expenses.

<br>**Event time line: Significance, Type of change(s), Description of
change(s)**:  

**1992**: Major; Introduced; New variable.

**1993**: Minor; Component; Excludes tax agents fees and superannuation
deductions.

**1996**: Minor; Component; Also includes Financial Institutions Duty
(FID) charged on salary, wage, allowance, pension or benefit income.

**2009**: Moderate; Component; Can no longer claim this deduction under
d\_wre\_car, d\_wre\_travel, d\_wre\_clothing, d\_wre\_other, or
is\_net\_rent. Also excludes any portable electronic device (such as a
laptop, portable printer, personal digital assistant, calculator, mobile
phone and portable GPS navigation receiver), computer software,
protective clothing, a briefcase or a tool of trade, which is primarily
for use in employment, if employer provided it, paid for it or
reimbursed any of its cost, and the benefit was exempt from fringe
benefits tax.
