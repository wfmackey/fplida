### crs\_pt\_frank\_cr

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Partnerships and trusts  
<br>**Question**:  
Share of franking credit from franked dividends  
<br>**Label**:  
Share of franking credit from franked dividends  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Amount of allowable franking credits that the taxpayer is entitled to
claim as a franking tax offset through a partnership or a trust (see
i\_div\_frank for definition of dividends and franking credit).  

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to the amount of allowable franking credits that
the taxpayer is entitled to claim as a franking tax offset from
partnership or trust (see i\_div\_frank for definition of dividends and
franking credit) distribution.  
Taxpayer can only claim a share of an imputation credit which relates to
the share of a franked dividend paid to a partnership or trust which is
indirectly included in the amount of non-primary production partnership
income or loss pt\_is\_pship\_dist\_npp), or in the amount of trust
income from non-primary production (pt\_is\_trust\_dist\_npp) or
non-primary production franked distribution from trusts
(pt\_is\_frank\_dist). Therefore, they cannot claim an imputation credit
for a dividend paid to the partnership or trust which was an exempt
distribution because family trust distribution tax or ultimate
beneficiary non-disclosure tax was paid on it.  
They cannot claim a share of an imputation credit through a trust in the
following circumstances:  
- the trust has an overall loss for the income year  
- did not show an amount of trust income from non-primary production
(pt\_is\_trust\_dist\_npp)  
- the amount of trust income from non-primary production
(pt\_is\_trust\_dist\_npp) is not attributable to the franked dividend
which has generated the imputation credit.  
  
To claim a share of imputation credit in relation to the share of a
franked dividend, the taxpayer must also be a ‘Qualified person’ by
meeting the holding period rule and related payments rule.  
However, taxpayer might not be entitled to claim the franking credits
if:  
- within 45 days of buying the shares (90 days for certain preference
shares), they either sold them or entered into an arrangement to reduce
the risk of making a loss on them (Holding period rule)  
- they were under an obligation to make, or were likely to make, a
related payment (Related payments rule), or  
- they received a dividend as a result of a dividend washing arrangement
(Dividend washing integrity rule).  
   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Record the share of franking credit from franked dividends.  

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1990-91, this variable refers to the total imputation credit from
franked dividends from a partnership or trust distribution.  

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1990-91, record the share of franking credit from franked
dividends.

<br>**Event time line: Significance, Type of change(s), Description of
change(s)**:  

**1997**: Moderate; Component; Missing pages in TaxPack.

**2001**: Moderate; Component; Holding period rule Unless they were
contractually obliged to acquire them before 7.30 p.m. Australian
Eastern Standard Time (AEST) on 13 May 1997, shareholders who acquired
shares on or after 1 July 1997 must own them for more than 45 days (not
counting the day of acquisition or disposal) before being entitled to a
franking tax offset from franked dividends paid or credited on the
shares. If the shares are preference shares the shareholder must hold
them for more than 90 days (not counting the day or acquisition or
disposal).  
  
Between 13 May 1997 - 30 June 1999, the holding period rule does not
apply to an individual shareholder whose entitlement to franking tax
offsets for the year from all franked dividends, received directly or
indirectly through partnerships or trusts, is $2000 or less. After 30
June 1999, the threshold increased to $5000 or less.  
  
Related payments rule  
The related payments rule prevents the investor from claiming a franking
tax offset on franked dividends or credited on shares if a related
payment is made under an arrangement entered into on or after 7.30 p.m.
AEST on 13 May 1997. This rule applies if the investor is under an
obligation to pass on the dividend to someone else and they are not
holding the shares ‘at risk’ around the dividend period.  
  

**2003**: Minor; Component; Recorded as both imputation and franking
credits.

**2004**: Minor; Component; Imputation is removed from definition
