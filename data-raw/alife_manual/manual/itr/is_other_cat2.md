### is\_other\_cat2

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Other income  
<br>**Question**:  
Type of income - Category 2  
<br>**Label**:  
Category 2 component of other income (pre-2018)  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Category 2 component of other income (pre-2018) not reported elsewhere
on the tax return form.   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
Latest year: 2016-17. Other income that is not reported elsewhere on the
tax return form is split into category 1 and category 2 type. Category 2
income includes any other income not described in category 1 (see
is\_other\_cat1).   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Latest year: 2016-17. Add up all category 2 income and write the
total.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1997-98, other income that is not reported elsewhere on the tax
return form is split into category 1 and category 2 type. Category 2
income includes:  
- Lump sum payments in arrears of Australian pensions or annuities
referred to at question 7: Other Australian pensions or annuities
(i\_annuities) and foreign pensions or annuities which have or had an
undeducted purchase price  
- Non-qualifying component of an eligible termination payment  
- Foreign exchange gains  
- Benefits from an employee share scheme  
- Royalties  
- Taxable scholarships, bursaries, grants or other education awards  
- Benefits or prizes from an investment-related lotteries  
- Eligible assessable income within the meaning of section 158H Income
Tax Assessment Act 1936 of an eligible person (e.g. artist, composer
inventor, etc)  
- Balancing adjustment profit from the disposal, loss or destruction of
the taxpayer’s car for which car expenses were claimed  
- Payments under a sickness or accidents insurance policy, except for
payments already shown at item 1 Gross salary and wages shown on group
certificate (i\_salary\_wage) or item 2 Allowances benefits, earnings,
tips, director’s fees, etc (i\_allowances).   - Interest from
infrastructure borrowings  
- Gains on disposal or redemption of traditional securities under
section 70B Income Tax Assessment Act 1936  
- Any other assessable income.  
   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1997-98, add up all category 2 income and write the total.

**1998**: Major; Introduced; New variable.

**2000**: Minor; Component; Includes:  
- Rebatable interest derived under the land transport facilities tax
rebate scheme  
- Jury duty fees unless the taxpayer has to pay the fees to the
taxpayer’s employer because the taxpayer received their normal
employment income whilst they were on jury duty.  
  

**2001**: Minor; Component; Described as any income not described in
category 1 (see TaxPack Supplement 2001 for more details).

**2018**: Major; Discontinued; Variable discontinued.
