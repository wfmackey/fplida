### pt\_is\_landcare\_exp\_npp

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Income  
<br>**Question**:  
Partnerships and trusts: Non-primary production: Landcare operations
expenses  
<br>**Label**:  
Landcare operations expenses related to distributions from non-primary
production partnership and trusts.   
<br>**General Notes**:  
Deduction for capital expenditure by primary producers and eligible
non-primary producers on conserving or conveying water or on the
prevention or treatment of land degradation.    

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to the deduction from eligible expenditure on
landcare operations. If a trustee incurred eligible expenditure on
landcare operations, only the trustee, not a beneficiary of the trust,
can claim deductions for that expenditure.   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Write the share of the total of any landcare operations expenditure that
relates to non-primary production income or loss from partnerships.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1997-98, this variable refers to any landcare operations expenses
which are deductible and were incurred in earning non-primary production
income.   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1997-98, write the share of the total of any landcare operations
expenditure that relates to non-primary production income or loss.
