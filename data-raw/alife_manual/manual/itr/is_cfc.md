### is\_cfc

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Income  
<br>**Question**:  
Foreign entities: income from a controlled foreign company  
<br>**Label**:  
Foreign entities: income from a controlled foreign company  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Total attributed income amount received by the taxpayer from controlled
foreign entities where they had direct controlling interest totalling
10% or more, or had effective control of the foreign company.  

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to Australian residents for tax purpose who had
either a direct or an indirect interest in a controlled foreign company
(CFC) such that they alone, or with associates, had direct or indirect
controlling interests totalling 10% or more, or had effective control of
the foreign company. Temporary residents do not need to show foreign
investment income from CFC.  

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Work out attributed foreign income from any CFC.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1996-97, this variable refers to Australian residents for tax
purpose who had either a direct or an indirect interest in a controlled
foreign company (CFC) such that they alone, or with associates, had
direct or indirect controlling interests totalling 10% or more, or had
effective control of the foreign company. Temporary residents do not
need to show foreign investment income from CFC.    

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1996-97, work out attributed foreign income from any CFC.
