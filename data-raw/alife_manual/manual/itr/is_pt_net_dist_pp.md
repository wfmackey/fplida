### is\_pt\_net\_dist\_pp

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Income  
<br>**Question**:  
Partnerships and trusts: Net primary production amount  
<br>**Label**:  
Net primary production income  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Net income from primary production activities.  

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to net income as a partner in a partnership that
derived primary production income and/or income received or entitled to
from a trust that made an income from primary production activities.  
This excludes:  
- attributed foreign income and any other foreign source income from a
partnership or trust (unless instructed otherwise), for example, in tax
return form 2017-18, see questions 19 and 20.  
- capital gain from a trust (unless instructed otherwise) (in tax return
form 2017-18, see questions 18)  
- capital gain or capital loss in respect of your interest in a
partnership or a partnership asset (in tax return form 2017-18, see
questions 18).  
- income from a corporate limited partnership (in tax return form
2017-18, see questions 11).  
- income from a public trading trust or a corporate unit trust (in tax
return form 2017-18, see questions 11).  
- interest you received, or were credited with, from a joint account,
where the individual tax file number was quoted to the financial
institution (in tax return form 2017-18, see questions 10).  
- that part of a distribution which relates to an amount of trust income
on which family trust distribution tax has been paid (in tax return form
2017-18, see questions A5) or trustee beneficiary non-disclosure tax has
been paid.  
- rent derived jointly (or in common) with another person from a jointly
held property where you were not a member of a partnership carrying on a
business of renting out properties (in tax return form 2017-18, see
questions 21).  
   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
(a) Write the share of primary production partnership income or loss
(excluding any attributed income or other foreign source income) at N:
Distribution from partnerships at item 13: Partnership and trusts.  
(b) Write the total share of primary production trust net income or loss
at L: Share of net income from trusts at item 13: Partnership and
trusts.   (c) Calculate the share of eligible expenditure on landcare
operations at I: Landcare operations and deduction for decline in value
of water facility, fencing asset and fodder storage asset at item 13:
Partnership and trusts.  
(d) Write at X: Other deductions relating to amounts shown at N and L.  
(e) To calculate net primary production income, sum (a), (b), and (c)
and take away the amounts at (d).  
   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1990-91, this variable refers to net income from primary production
received as a partner in a partnership or as a beneficiary of a trust. A
trust includes cash management, property and other units trusts.   This
excludes:  
- any dividends or any other share of income from a corporate unit trust
or a public trading trust, this is included at Question 18: Dividends
(i\_div)  
- any capital gain or loss, this is included at Question 14: Net capital
gain (is\_cg\_net)  
- any foreign source income, this is included at Question 15: General
net foreign source income (isn\_fsi\_exempt\_empl)  
- any attributed foreign income, this is included at Question 16:
Attributed foreign income (is\_cfc).  
   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1990-91, sum all income from primary production earned from a
partnership or a trust less any deductions not claimed in the
partnership or trust return.

<br>**Event time line: Significance, Type of change(s), Description of
change(s)**:  

**1992**: Minor; Component; Includes any dividend plus any imputation
credit attached to that dividend.

**1995**: Major; Question; Is attributed to label N Distribution from
primary production income at item 10 Partnership and trusts, not the net
primary production income from partnership and trusts.

**1996**: Major; Question; Returned to net primary production income,
which is calculated as the total share of primary production reported at
N (pt\_is\_pship\_dist\_pp) minus total deductions not already claimed
by the partnership or trust, against this income or loss at X Deduction
from primary production income distribution (pt\_is\_ded\_pp)

**1997**: Major; TaxPack; Question 10 Partnership and trusts missing
from TaxPack 1997.

**1998**: Major; Calculation; Calculated as sum of label N Distribution
from primary production partnerships (not including foreign source
income) and label L Distribution from primary production trusts (not
including net capital gains and foreign source income) less label I
Landcare operations and water conservation expenses and label Y Other
deductions relating to distributions.

**2002**: Moderate; Calculation; Landcare operations deduction also
include deduction for decline in value of water facility.

**2016**: Moderate; Calculation; Landcare operations deduction also
include deduction for decline in value of fencing asset and storage
asset.
