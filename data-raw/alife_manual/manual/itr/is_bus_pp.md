### is\_bus\_pp

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Income  
<br>**Question**:  
Net income or loss from business: Primary production  
<br>**Label**:  
Net income or loss from primary production business  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Net primary production income or loss amount from business activities.
Reported by taxpayers that are required to complete the business and
professional items schedule.  
  
For income tax purposes " Primary Production " refers to a business of "
Farming “, and hence the term” Primary Producer “;” Non-Primary
Production " refers to a business that is not related to " Farming ".  
  
Primary production business activities include:   - cultivating or
propagating plants, fungi or their products or parts (including seeds,
spores, bulbs and similar things) in any physical environment.   -
manufacturing dairy produce from raw material that \[the taxpayer\]
produced;   - felling trees in a planation or forest and planting or
tending trees in a plantation or forest that are intended to be
felled.  
- maintaining animals for the purpose of selling them or their bodily
produce, including natural increase.    
The relevant indicators used by the ATO in determining whether an
activity is a business of primary production are outlined in Taxation
Ruling TR 97/11 - Income Tax: am I carrying on a business of primary
production?   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to income or loss derived from primary production
activities.  
  
Primary production specific income includes income from:  
- cultivating or propagating plants, fungi or their products or parts
(including seeds, spores, bulbs and similar things) in any physical
environment  
- maintaining animals for the purpose of selling them or their bodily
produce, including natural increase  
- manufacturing dairy produce from raw material  
- fishing or pearling  
- tree farming or felling.  
   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
The net income/loss from primary production is recorded under item P8:
Business income and expenses on page 11 as total business income net
total expenses taking into account reconciliation items and deferred
non-comercial business losses from previous year.  
  
Total business income includes: P8-D: Gross payments where Australian
business number not quoted; P8-B: Gross payments subject to foreign
resident withholding (excluding capital gains); P8-F: Gross payments -
voluntary agreement; P8-O: Gross payments - labour; P8-H: assessable
government industry payments; P8-J: Other business expenses.  
  
Total business expenses includes: Opening cost of sales, foreign
resident withholding expenses (excluding capital gains), contractor,
subcontractor and commission expenses, superannuation expenses, bad
debts, lease expenses, rent expenses, interest expenses within
Australian, interest expenses overseas, depreciation expenses, motor
vehicle expenses, repairs and maintenance, and all other expenses.  
  
Reconciliation items include: Section 40-880 deduction, business
deduction for project pool, landcare operations and business deduction
for decline in value of water facility, fencing asset and fodder storage
asset, income reconciliation adjustment, expense reconciliation
adjustments.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1990-91, this variable refers to any income or loss from primary
production activities. Primary production includes production from
cultivating the land, keeping and breeding animals or poultry to sell
them or their produce, fishing operation, forest operations, and
manufacture of dairy produce by the dairy farmer.   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1990-91, the net income/loss from primary production activities is
calculated as the gross primary production business income minus total
primary business deductions.  
  
Gross primary production is transferred from the ‘Income Tax and primary
producers’ notes and worksheet’.  
  
Total primary production deductions include salary and wages paid,
payment to contractors, depreciation of assets, interest paid on money
borrowed for business use, motor vehicle expenses, repairs, lease
payments and other business related expenses related to primary
production.

<br>**Event time line: Significance, Type of change(s), Description of
change(s)**:  

**1992**: Minor; Component; Total primary production business income
includes total business income from primary production during the income
year. This also includes profit made on disposal of depreciable assets,
sales of trading stock, service income and fees, delivery charges and
commission revenue related to primary production business.

This excludes:  
- Dividend income, reported at Question 19 Dividends (i\_div\_frank and
i\_div\_unfrank)  
- Income from rent, reported at Question 18 Rent, premiums, etc
(isn\_gross\_rent)  
- Income from capital gains, reported at Question 15 Net capital gain
(is\_cg\_net)  
- Royalties   - Interest income, reported at Question 10 Interest
(i\_interest) - Attributed foreign income, reported at Question 16
Attributed foreign income (is\_cfc).  
- Foreign source income, reported at Question 17 General net foreign
source income (is\_fsi\_other\_foreign\_incl\_rent).  
- Foreign exchange profit/loss   - Income from a partnership or trust,
reported at Question 11 Partnership and trusts
(is\_pt\_net\_dist\_npp).  
  
Calculation Business income and deductions for primary and non-primary
production are now recorded in the Business/professional declaration
items Item 44 Business income and deductions on page 4.

Expense categories are recorded separately and include: External labour
costs (44E), cost of sales (44F), total interest expenses (44G),
depreciation expenses (44H), motor vehicle expenses (44I), repairs and
maintenances (44J), land degradation expenses (44K) and all other
expenses (44L). Employers are also entitled to a deduction for
contributions made to a superannuation, provident, benefit, or
retirement fund for the benefit of eligible employees or their
dependents. This is reported under all other expenses (44L).

Net income/loss from primary production business is calculated as total
business income less expenses add reconciliation adjustments (44N).
Reconciliation adjustments reconciles the business operating profit or
loss with the business taxable income.

**1993**: Minor; Component; Expenses categories added include:
Superannuation category (48G) and separate categories for lease (48J)
and rent expenses (48K) added under Item 48 Business income and
deductions on page 4.

**1994**: Minor; Component; Investment allowances is added to net
income/loss from business under item 47 Business income and deductions
(47R). Investment allowances is calculated as 10% of the capital cost of
acquiring or constructing new items of plant which costs $3,000 or more
and quality for the general investment allowance. This is added to net
income/loss from business.

**1995**: Minor; Component; Also includes those who received income
under the reportable payment system.

Any taxable income earned as an artist, composer, inventor, performer,
production associate, active sportsperson or writer must also include
their eligible taxable income at Question 19 Other income - eligible
taxable income (see isn\_prof\_taxable\_inc). This amount will only be
taxed once.

**1996**: Minor; Component; Investment allowance discontinued from item
54 Business income and deductions.

**1997**: Minor; Component; Contractor, sub-contractor and commission
expenses added to item 54 Business Income and deductions on page 6.

Investment allowances added back in to item 54 Business income and
deductions.

**1998**: Moderate; Component; An activity is generally defined to be
business in nature if it:  
- has actually commenced  
- has a significant commercial purpose or character  
- is undertaken in a business-like manner  
- results in a profit  
- is carried out in a manner that is characteristic of the industry  
- has repetition, regularity or continuity  
- cannot be more properly described as a hobby.    
Total business income from primary production is the sum gross RPS
income (P6F), assessable government industry payments (P6H), and other
business income (P6J) recorded under Item P6 Business income and
expenses under 1998 Business and professional items schedule.

Gross reportable payment system (RPS) income is the total amount of RPS
income derived from primary and non-primary production. RPS applies to
certain payments made in the fishing, clothing, smash repair, and from 1
March 1997, fruit and vegetable industries.

Any assessable government industry payments, include any payments that
are assessable income received from government sources that has been
paid as a result of non-primary business activities such as bounties,
diesel fuel rebate, drought relief, employee subsidies, export incentive
grants, Medicare payments to medical practices.

Other business income includes gross sales of trading stock, goods taken
for own use from stock, gross earnings from services, bad debt
recovered, profit on sale of depreciable assets, royalties, insurance
recoveries, subsidies, employee contributions for fringe benefits, and
non-assessable government assistance from all sources.

Expenses Interest expenses split into interest expenses within Australia
(P6Q) and interest expenses earned overseas (P6R) under Item P6 Business
income and expenses under 1998 Business and professional items schedule.

Reconciliation items include drought investment allowances (P6U)
calculated as 10% of the cost of new property to fight the effects of
drought (limit of $5,000), environment protection or environmental
impact study expenses (P6V), landcare operations and water
conservation/conveying expenses (P6W) and other reconciliation
adjustments (P6X).

**2000**: Minor; Component; Reconciliation item ‘other reconciliation
adjustments’ split into income reconciliation adjustments (P6X) and
expense reconciliation adjustment (P6H). Income reconciliation
adjustments includes income add backs - income not shown in the profit
or loss statement but is assessable and income subtractions - income
shown in the profit or loss statement but is not assessable income.
Expense reconciliation adjustments include expense add backs - expenses
shown in the accounts which are not tax deductible and expense
subtractions - items shown as expenses which are deductible for expense
purposes.

**2001**: Major; Component; New categorisation of primary production
income reported under item P8 business income and expenses on page 10.
New categories include:  
- income under a Pay As You Go (PAYG) voluntary agreement (P8F)  
- income from which an amount was withheld because they did not quote
their Australian Business Number (ABN) (P8D)  
- income as an independent contractor under a labour hire arrangement or
income from a specified payment including payment for tutorial services
provided for the Aboriginal Tutorial Assistance Scheme of the Department
of Education, Training and Youth Affairs, and payment for translation
and interpretation services for the Translating and Interpreting Service
of the Department of Immigration and Multicultural Affairs (P8O)   If
they are self-employed, or driving a taxi under a standard bailment
agreement with an owner/operator.  
Excludes any income reported under Question 13 Net personal services
income (is\_psi\_net).  
  
Primary production business includes:  
- cultivating or propagating plants, fungi or their products or parts  
- maintaining animals for the purpose of selling them or their bodily
produce  
- manufacturing dairy produce from raw material you produced  
- conducting operations relating directly to taking or catching fish,
turtles, dugong, bêche-de-mer, crustaceans or aquatic molluscs  
- conducting operations relating directly to taking or culturing pearls
or pearl shell  
- planting or tending trees in a plantation or forest that are intended
to be felled  
- felling trees in a plantation or forest  
- transporting trees felled in a plantation or forest to the place where
they are first to be milled or processed or from which they are to be
transported to the place where they are first to be milled or
processed.  
  

**2002**: Minor; Component; Section 80-880 deduction (P8A) and business
deduction for project pool (P8L) added to reconciliation items and
Environmental impact assessment and environmental protection expenses
changed to Deduction for environmental protection expenses at item P8
Business income and expenses on page 10.

**2003**: Minor; Component; Income from a specified payment includes
income as a performing artist in a promotional activity. Other business
income includes those received as a sole trader.

Drought investment allowance removed from reconciliation items in item
P8 Business income and expenses.

**2005**: Minor; Component; Gross payments subject to foreign resident
withholding (P8B) is added to income and foreign withholding expenses
(P8U) is added to expenses under P8 Business income and expenses on page
10.

If the taxpayer was 55 years or older on 30 June 2005, they may be
entitled to the mature age worker tax offset. Any business income or
loss will need to be reported at M Question T12 Net income from working

**2006**: Minor; Component; If the taxpayer derived income from business
under the simplified tax system (STS), they may be eligible to claim the
entrepreneurs tax offset. Net STS income is reported under N item T14
Entrepreneurs tax offset - net simplified tax system income.

**2008**: Minor; Component; If the taxpayer derived income from a
business and quality for the small business entity concession, they may
be eligible to claim the entrepreneurs tax offset. Generally a sole
trader, partnership or trust is a small business entity for the 2007-08
income year if its aggregated turnover was less than $2 million.

**2009**: Minor; Component; Small business and general business tax
break in the form of an investment allowance is (P8F) added to
Reconciliation items at item P8 Business income and expenses on page 10.

**2013**: Minor; Component; Deduction for environmental protection
expenses, small business and general business tax break removed from
Reconciliation items under item P8 Business income and expenses on page
12.
