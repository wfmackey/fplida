### ml\_full\_exempt\_cd

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Medicare levy related items  
<br>**Question**:  
Medicare levy reduction or exemption: Exemption categories - Full
medicare levy exemption: claim type  
<br>**Label**:  
Claim type for the full Medicare levy exemption  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Claim type for full Medicare levy exemption.   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable is a binary indicator which indicates whether the taxpayer
is a temporary resident for Medicare purposes and have a Medicare
entitlement statement from Services Australia given that they are
eligible for the full levy exemption for the period. Therefore, they can
claim a full exemption from the 2.0% Medicare levy.   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
If the taxpayer was a temporary resident for Medicare purposes and have
a Medicare entitlement statement from Services Australia, then the code
letter is C. If they do not fall within this category the code letter is
blank.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1999-2000, this variable is an indicator if the taxpayer has a
certificate from the Levy Exemption Certification Unit and are not
entitled to Medicare benefits. Therefore, they are able to claim the
full exemption on the 1.5% Medicare levy.   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1999-2000, if the taxpayer has a certificate from the Levy Exemption
Certification Unit and are not entitled to Medicare benefits, print the
letter C. Otherwise leave the box blank.
