ATO: Excess non-concessional contribution associated earnings  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Excess non-concessional contribution associated earnings.  
  
If your non-concessional superannuation contribution exceeds your
non-concessional contributions cap then you have excess non-concessional
contribution.  
  
The associated earnings is an amount calculated by the ATO to
approximate the amount earned from the excess non-concessional
contributions while in the fund.
