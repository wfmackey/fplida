### on\_eto\_net\_inc

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Tax offsets  
<br>**Question**:  
Entrepreneurs tax offset  
<br>**Label**:  
Net small business entity income  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Net small business entity income amount reported by the taxpayer.   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
Latest year: 2011-12. This variable refers to the net small business
income for a taxpayer who is eligible to claim the entrepreneurs tax
offset (ETO). The entrepreneurs tax offset (ETO) is a tax offset equal
to 25% of the income tax liability attributable to the net small
business income of a small business entity (either as a sole trader,
partnership or trust) with an aggregated turnover of $50,000 or less.
This tax offset starts to phase out when the small business entity’s
aggregated turnover exceeds $50,000 and ceases when the aggregated
turnover reaches $75,000.   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Latest year: 2011-12. Write the amount of share of the net small
business income. For a sole trader this is the total small business
entity turnover net allowable deductions.    

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 2005-06, this variable refers to the net income calculated under the
simplified tax system (STS) for a taxpayer who is eligible for the
entrepreneurs tax offset. The entrepreneurs tax offset (ETO) is a tax
offset equal to 25% of the income tax liability attributable to the net
STS income of a small business entity (either as a sole trader,
partnership or trust) with an annual STS group turnover under a
threshold.   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 2005-06, write the amount of net simplified tax system (STS) income.
For a sole trader this is the total STS annual turnover net allowable
deductions.
