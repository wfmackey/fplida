### i\_annuities

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Income  
<br>**Question**:  
Other Australian pensions or annuities: including superannuation
pension  
<br>**Label**:  
Gross payments of other Australian pensions or annuities - including
superannuation pension  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Gross payments of other Australian pensions or annuities - including
superannuation pension.  
  
Reporting of superannuation income stream benefits significantly changed
from the 2008 income year, with taxpayers required to report both the
taxed element and untaxed elements of the taxable income component of
the income stream benefit.  
From the 2008 income year onwards, the values previously captured in the
variable i\_annuities were separated into new variables
i\_annuities\_txd and i\_annuities\_untxd as the taxed and untaxed
elements were separately identified for the first time.  
Note: The superannuation income stream benefits amount classified as the
tax-free component is non-assessable non-exempt income and not included
in the calculation of taxable income.  
  
  

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
Latest year: 2006-07. This variable refers to income from Australian
annuity, superannuation, or other pensions and therefore may be eligible
to receive a rebate on taxed payments. Australian annuity,
superannuation, or other pensions include:  
- superannuation and similar pensions and annuities paid by an
Australian superannuation fund, life issurance company or retirement
savings account (RSA) provider, and  
- pensions paid by a fund established for the benefit of Commonwealth,
state or territory employees and their dependents - for example funds
managed by ComSuper or VIcSuper.  
  
Does not include pensions recorded under question 6: Australian
Government pensions and allowances (i\_gov\_pension).  
Deductions and tax offsets eligible on annuity payments include:  
- deduction on the undeducted purchase price of an Australian annuity
recorded under d\_aust\_pension\_upp  
- senior Australian tax offset, see on\_sato\_cd  
- tax offset on superannuation annuity or pensions recorded under
onra\_super\_inc\_stream.  
   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Latest year: 2006-07: gross amounts of Australian annuity,
superannuation, or pensions.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1990-91, this variable refers to income from annuity,
superannuation, or other pensions that does not include pensions
recorded under Section 2 Pension or other benefit or allowances
(i\_gov\_pension), exempt income, non-taxable pensions, and therefore
may be eligible to receive a rebate on taxed payments. The rebate may be
claimed on taxable annuities or pensions and must have started payments
from or after 1 July 1988. Pensions from Commonwealth Superannuation
Fund do not qualify for a rebate unless the taxpayer did not opt to take
the lump sum payment on retirement. Gross payments is recorded under
this questions and rebate is automatically calculated for the taxpayer
by the ATO.  

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1990-91, gross amounts of claimable annuity, superannuation, or
pensions.

<br>**Event time line: Significance, Type of change(s), Description of
change(s)**:  

**1992**: Major; Component; Applicable foreign pensions or annuities are
recorded under Item 17 General net foreign source income
(is\_fsi\_empl\_pension or is\_fsi\_other\_foreign\_incl\_rent).

**1993**: Minor; Component; Taxpayer has the option to report rebate
amounts under Section 41 Other rebates (onra\_other\_non\_ref).

**1995**: Moderate; Component; Australian pensions and annuities
include:  
- superannuation, or similar pensions and annuities paid by Australian
superannuation funds, registered organisations or life companies.  
- pensions paid by funds established for the benefit of Commonwealth,
State or Territory employees and their dependants including Defence
Force Retirement Benefit Act pensions.  

**1996**: Moderate; Question; Question changed from “Other pension or
annuity” to “Other Australian pensions or annuities”. Rebates on
pensions and annuities are now recorded under Section 37 Superannuation
Rebates (onra\_super\_inc\_stream).

**1999**: Minor; Component; Taxpayer is now eligible for low income aged
pension rebate (see on\_sato\_cd).

**2001**: Minor; Question; Question changed from “Other Australian
pensions or annuities” to “Other Australian pensions or annuities -
including superannuation pensions”.

**2007**: Major; Split / Merge; Change in superannuation income
reporting, variable split into the taxed element and the untaxed
elements of the taxable income component of the income stream benefit.

**2008**: Major; Discontinued; Variable discontinued.
