### or\_phi

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Private health insurance policy details  
<br>**Question**:  
Your Australian Government rebate received.   
<br>**Label**:  
Total private health insurance Australian Government rebate received.   
<br>**General Notes**:  
Private health insurance rebate amount.  
  
Note: from the 2014 income year, the private health insurance rebate is
no longer payable on the lifetime health cover loading component of
PHIP.   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to the total Australian Government rebate received
as a non-dependent on a private health insurance policy or a payee for a
dependent-child-only policy.   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Transfer the Australian Government rebate received amount as shown on
the Private health insurance statement.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 2012-13, this variable refers to the total Australian Government
rebate received as a non-dependent on a private health insurance policy
or a payee for a dependent-child-only policy.   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 2012-13, transfer the Australian Government rebate received amount
as shown on the Private health insurance statement.

<br>**Event time line: Significance, Type of change(s), Description of
change(s)**:  

**2013**: Major; Introduced; New Variable. Introduction of a single
rebate adjustment factor on PHI rebates.  
  
Major; Wording; Question wording changed to included appropiate level of
coverage

**2014**: Major; Component; From 1 July 2013, the Australian Government
private health insurance rebate is no longer payable on the lifetime
health cover loading component of the private health insurance premiums.
This only applies for those 31 years or older and who do not have
private patient hospital cover.  
Amount recorded at label J is now adjusted to remove the lifetime health
cover loading amount.
