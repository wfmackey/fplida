### d\_aust\_pension\_upp

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Deductions  
<br>**Question**:  
Deductible amount of undeducted purchase price (UPP) of an Australian
pension or annuity  
<br>**Label**:  
Deductible amount of undeducted purchase price (UPP) of an Australian
pension or annuity  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Deduction claim amount for pension or annuity contributions for which a
deduction has not previously been claimed.  
  
The undeducted purchase price (UPP) is the amount contributed towards
the purchase price of the pension or annuity for which a tax deduction
could not or has not been claimed. The deductible amount, calculated
using UPP, can be used to reduce yearly taxable income from pension or
annuity.   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
Latest year: 2006-07. If taxpayers showed income from an Australian
pension or annuity at item 7: ‘Other Australian pensions or annuities -
including superannuation pensions’ (i\_annuities) on tax return, they
may be able to reduce the amount of taxable income that they have to pay
tax on if the Australian pension or annuity has a UPP.  
  
A pension or annuity may have an undeducted purchase price if
taxpayer:  
- received a superannuation pension and could not claim for some or all
of the personal contributions made to their superannuation fund or
retirement savings account provider in previous years  
- received a pension or annuity that reverted to them on the death of
another person  
- bought their own annuity or pension with their own capital.  
  
Note: This does not include any Commonwealth of Australia government
pension reported at Item 6.    

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Latest year: 2006-07. Total of all deductible amount of UPP for all
Australian pension or annuity.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1997-98, if taxpayers showed income from an Australian pension or
annuity at item 7: ‘Other Australian pensions or annuities’
(i\_annuities) on tax return, they may be able to reduce the amount of
taxable income that they have to pay tax on if the Australian pension or
annuity has a UPP.  
  
A pension or annuity may have an undeducted purchase price if
taxpayer:  
- received a superannuation pension and could not claim for all the
personal contributions made to their superannuation fund or retirement
savings account provider in previous years  
- received a pension or annuity that reverted to them on the death of
another person  
- bought their own annuity or pension.  
Note: This does not include any Commonwealth of Australia government
pension reported at item 6.  
   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1997-98, total of all deductible amount of UPP for all Australian
pension or annuity.
