### sp\_rept\_fringe\_benefit

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Spouse details - married or de facto  
<br>**Question**:  
Your spouse’s total reportable fringe benefits amounts.   
<br>**Label**:  
Spouse’s total reportable fringe benefits amounts.   
<br>**General Notes**:  
Spouse reportable fringe benefits amount (RFBA). The RFBA amount is
included in the calculation of taxable income to determine the taxpayers
liability for the Medicare levy surcharge and HELP repayments, in
addition to entitlement for superannuation co-contribution payments and
several tax offsets.  
  
From income year 2018, this question split into RFBA from employers
exempt from FBT under section 57A of the FBTAA 1986
(sp\_rept\_fringe\_benefit\_exe) and from employers not exempt from FBT
under section 57A of the FBTAA 1986
(sp\_rept\_fringe\_benefit\_nexe).   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
Latest year: 2016-17. This variable refers to spouse’s total reportable
fringe benefit amounts.   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Latest year: 2016-17. Write down the spouse’s total reportable fringe
benefit amount.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1999-2000, this variable refers to the spouse’s total reportable
fringe benefits amounts, which is used to calculate Medicare levy
surcharge or the tax offset for superannuation contributions on behalf
of spouse.   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1999-2000, write down spouse’s total reportable fringe benefit
amount.

<br>**Event time line: Significance, Type of change(s), Description of
change(s)**:  

**2000**: Major; Introduced; New variable.

**2010**: Major; Component; Taxpayer must provide spouse’s RFBA to
calculate spouse tax offset, pensioners and seniors tax offset, Medicare
levy surcharge, and tax offsets for superannuation contributions on
behalf of spouse, parent, spouse’s parent or invalid relative, and the
entrepreneurs tax offset.

**2013**: Major; Component; If taxpayer had a spouse during the income
year they must fill out all details in Spouse details - married or de
facto.
