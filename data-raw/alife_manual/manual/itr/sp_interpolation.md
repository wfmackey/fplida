<br>**General Notes**:  
This variable attempts to fill gaps in spouse information where
information did not need to be provided but where sufficient evidence
exists to suggest that the tax payer did have a spouse. Prior to the
2013 tax return form, only a subset of the lodging population were
required to report details for their spouse.  
  
Where the same spouse appears to be present in earlier and later
periods, but no information was provided in between, it is assumed that
the spousal relationship was continuous throughout that period. The
interpolation algorithm draws on information provided about a spouse in
earlier and later periods, including their first initial, TFN, and birth
date. Additionally a spouse matching procedure was applied and where the
taxpayer was reported to be the spouse of someone, the taxpayer has been
interpolated as having a spouse.
