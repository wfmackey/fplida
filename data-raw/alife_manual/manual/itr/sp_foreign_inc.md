### sp\_foreign\_inc

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Spouse details - married or de facto  
<br>**Question**:  
Your spouse’s target foreign income  
<br>**Label**:  
Total spouse’s target foreign income  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Total foreign income amounts earned, derived or received from sources
outside Australia by the taxpayers’ spouse, that are not included in the
spouse’s taxable income, nor received in the form of a fringe benefit.  
  
Foreign currency amounts are converted and reported in Australian
dollars.   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to target foreign income received by the taxpayer’s
spouse. This includes any income amount earned, derived or received from
sources outside Australia for the spouse’s own use or benefit that is
neither included in the spouse’s taxable income nor received in the form
of a fringe benefit, or periodic payments or benefits by way of gifts or
allowances. Spouse is defined as a person (of any sex) who was in a
relationship with the taxpayer that was registered under a prescribed
state or territory law, or although not legally married, live together
on a genuine domestic basis in a relationship as a couple.   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Write the target foreign income received by the spouse.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 2009-10, this variable refers to target foreign income received by
the taxpayer’s spouse. This includes any income amount earned, derived
or received from sources outside Australia for the spouse’s own use or
benefit that is neither included in the spouse’s taxable income nor
received in the form of a fringe benefit, or periodic payments or
benefits by way of gifts or allowances. The taxpayer must fill out this
question if they completed T1 Spouse (without dependent child or
student), child-housekeeper or housekeeper tax offset, or T10 Parent,
spouse’s parent or invalid relative tax offset. Spouse is defined as a
person (of any sex) who was in a relationship with the taxpayer that was
registered under a prescribed state or territory law, or although not
legally married, live together on a genuine domestic basis in a
relationship as a couple.   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 2009-10, write the target foreign income received by the spouse.
