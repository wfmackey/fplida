### is\_attributed\_foreign\_income

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Income  
<br>**Question**:  
Attributed foreign income  
<br>**Label**:  
Total foreign attributed income  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Total foreign attributed income including distribution from a
partnership or trust which includes attributed foreign income, interest
in a foreign company or foreign trust, transferred property or services
to a non-resident trust at any time and interest in a foreign life
insurance policy. From 1997, this variable splits into is\_cfc,
is\_transferor\_trust\_inc and is\_foreign\_invest.  

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
Latest year: 1995-96. This variable refers to attributed foreign income
earned by the taxpayer if they are an Australian resident.  
  
Attributed foreign income include:  
- income from a partnership or trust,   - income from a foreign company
or foreign trust, where the taxpayer had an associate-inclusive control
interest of 10 per cent or more in that foreign company or foreign
trust, or had effective control of a foreign company alone or with
associates  
- income from transferring property or services to a non-resident trust
  - or income from a foreign life insurance policy.    
These interest amounts may be subject to tax on an accruals basis.  
  

Your associate-inclusive control interest in a foreign company is the
sum of: - your direct control interests in the foreign company - your
indirect control interests in the foreign company - the direct and
indirect control interests of your associates in the foreign company.  

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Calculate the total attributed foreign income.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1990=91, this variable refers to attributed foreign income earned by
the taxpayer if they are an Australian resident.  
  
Attributed foreign income include:  
- income from a partnership or trust,   - income from a foreign company
or foreign trust, where the taxpayer had an associate-inclusive control
interest of 10 per cent or more in that foreign company or foreign
trust, or had effective control of a foreign company alone or with
associates  
- income from transferring property or services to a non-resident trust
  - or income from a foreign life insurance policy.    
These interest amounts may be subject to tax on an accruals basis.  
  

Your associate-inclusive control interest in a foreign company is the
sum of: - your direct control interests in the foreign company - your
indirect control interests in the foreign company - the direct and
indirect control interests of your associates in the foreign company.  

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1990-91, sum attributed income from an interest in a foreign company
or trust, transferred property or service to a trust which, during the
income year, was a non-resident trust, and the attributed foreign
component of a partnership or trust distribution.
