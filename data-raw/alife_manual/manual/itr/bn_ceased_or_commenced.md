### bn\_ceased\_or\_commenced

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Business and professional items section  
<br>**Question**:  
Status of your business - print X in one box only: Ceased
business/Commenced business  
<br>**Label**:  
Business status  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Code for taxpayer’s business status.  

-   Code Definition:\*  
    **15:** Multiple businesses  
    **10:** Commenced business  
    **5:** Ceased business  
      
      

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable indicates the status of the taxpayer’s business.  
  
*Code Definition*  
**10:** Commenced business.  
**5:** Ceased business.  
   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
If the taxpayer,  
- ceased their main business, enter X at C1Ceased business (in tax form
2017-18, it is Item P4)  
- commenced a new business, enter X at C2 Commenced business (item P4 in
tax form 2017-18)  
- had more than one option apply, enter X at C1 Ceased business, only,
and   - if neither option applies, leave both boxes blank.  
   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1990-91, this variable indicates the status of the taxpayer’s
business or businesses.  
  
*Code Definition*  
**15:** Multiple businesses  
**10:** Commenced business  
**5:** Ceased business  
   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1990-91, if the taxpayer,  
- had multiple business, i.e. income from more than one business
activity, place X in the box at B1 Multiple business,  
- ceased their business, place X in the box at B2 Ceased business,   -
commenced a business, place X in the box at B3 Commenced business.
