<br>**Label**:  
ATO: Lodgement channel type  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Lodgement channel through which the individual tax return has been
lodged into the Tax Office.  
  
*Categories Description*  
**TELE:** Telephone lodgement of tax return form.  
**AGNT\_PPR:** Paper tax return form prepared by a registered tax
agent.  
**SP\_MYTAX:** MyTax electronic lodgement with pre-filled information
self prepared by the taxpayer.  
**AGNT\_ELS:** Electronic lodgement service lodged by a registered tax
agent.  
**TXPK\_EXP:** TaxPack express keyed by Australia Post.  
**SP\_PPR:** Paper tax return form self prepared by the taxpayer.  
**ETAX:** E-tax online lodgement, discontinued 1 July 2016.
