### onra\_spouse\_housekeeper

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Tax offsets  
<br>**Question**:  
Spouse (without dependent child or student), child-housekeeper or
housekeeper.   
<br>**Label**:  
Spouse, child-housekeeper or housekeeper tax offset - available
amount.   
<br>**General Notes**:  
Spouse (without dependent child or student), child-housekeeper or
housekeeper tax offset available amount to claim, abolished in 2015.   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
Latest year: 2011-12. This variable refers to the spouse (without
dependent child or student), child-housekeeper or housekeeper tax offset
amount (see
<https://www.ato.gov.au/Individuals/Tax-return/2012/In-detail/Tax-return/Tax-offset-questions-T1-T6/T1---Spouse-(without-dependent-child-or-student),-child-housekeeper-or-housekeeper-2012/>
for more details).   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Latest year: 2011-12. Write the total amount of spouse (without
dependent child or student), child-housekeeper or housekeeper tax offset
available to claim.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1991-92, this variable refers to the spouse (married or de facto),
child-housekeeper or housekeeper rebate. The amount of rebate claimed
depends on separate net income (SNI) and whether the taxpayer had
dependent child or student. See TaxPack 1992 for more details on
eligibility and calculation.   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1991-92, calculate the amount that can be claimed.

<br>**Event time line: Significance, Type of change(s), Description of
change(s)**:  

**1992**: Major; Introduced; New variable.

**1995**: Moderate; Component; Home Child Care Allowance introduced for
couples with dependent children. Any payments that taxpayer’s spouse
receives will reduce the amount of dependent spouse rebate.

**1996**: Moderate; Component; Home Child Care Allowance incorporated
into the new social security payments, called Parenting Allowance, for
couples with dependent children. Any Parenting Allowance received by
taxpayer’s spouse will be excluded from their separate net income,
thereby reducing spouse rebate claimed.

**2001**: Major; Question; Question changed from “Spouse - married or de
facto - child-housekeeper or housekeeper” to “Spouse (without dependent
child or student), child-housekeeper or housekeeper”. The “with-child”
rate of tax offset has been incorporated into family tax benefit (FTB)
and is no longer reported under onra\_spouse\_housekeeper. Spouse or
child-housekeeper tax offset can only be claimed for the part of the
year the taxpayer or their spouse did not receive FTB or the period the
dependent child was not under the taxpayer’s care.

**2012**: Moderate; Legislative; Additional restrictions on claim
eligibility imposed from 1 July 2012, only taxpayers with a spouse born
before 1 July 1971 are entitled to claim the offset.

**2014**: Moderate; Legislative; Additional restrictions on claim
eligibility imposed from 1 July 2012, only taxpayers’ with a spouse born
before 1 July 1952 are entitled to claim the offset.

**2015**: Major; Discontinued; Variable discontinued.
