### sp\_net\_invest\_loss

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Spouse details - married or de facto  
<br>**Question**:  
Your spouse’s total net investment loss (total of net financial
investment loss and net rental property loss).   
<br>**Label**:  
Spouse’s total net investment loss (total of net financial investment
loss and net rental property loss).   
<br>**General Notes**:  
The spouse’s total net investment loss (total of net financial
investment loss and net rental property loss).   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to spouse’s total net investment loss including any
net rental property loss. See variables it\_invest\_loss and
it\_property\_loss for more details.   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Calculate spouse’s total net investment loss.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 2009-10, this variable refers to spouse’s total net investment loss
including any net rental property loss. See variables it\_invest\_loss
and it\_property\_loss for more details.   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 2009-10, calculate spouse’s total net investment loss.
