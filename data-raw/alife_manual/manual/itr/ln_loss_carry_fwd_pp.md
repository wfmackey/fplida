### ln\_loss\_carry\_fwd\_pp

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Losses  
<br>**Question**:  
Tax losses of earlier income years: Primary production losses carried
forward from earlier income years.  
<br>**Label**:  
Total primary production losses from earlier years available for use
this year.   
<br>**General Notes**:  
Total primary production losses from earlier years available for use
this year.   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to total primary production losses from earlier
years available for use this year. It is primary production losses
carried forward from earlier income years net exempt income (all or
part) of the current year.   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
For primary production, for example in tax form 2017-18, see question
L1:  
(a) Calculate the primary production losses from earlier years.  
(b) Calculate net exempt income in current year used to offset prior
year primary production loss.  
(c) Take (b) away from (a). This is total primary production losses from
earlier years available for use this year (ln\_loss\_carry\_fwd\_pp).  
   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 2004-05, this variable refers to total tax losses of earlier income
years from primary production available to deduct from net Australian
source income this year.   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 2004-05, for primary production:  
(a) Caluclate primary production tax losses from earlier income years.  
(b) Calculate net exempt income in current year used to offset prior
year primary production loss.  
(c) Take (b) away from (a).  
(d) Calculate the amount of prior year primary production loss used to
offset current year foreign source income.  
(e) Take (d) away from (c). This is total primary production tax losses
from earlier income years available to deduct from net Australian source
income this year.
