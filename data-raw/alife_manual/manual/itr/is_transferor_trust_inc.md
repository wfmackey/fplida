### is\_transferor\_trust\_inc

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Income  
<br>**Question**:  
Foreign entities: Transferor trust income  
<br>**Label**:  
Total attributed income amount received by the taxpayer under the
transferor trust measures  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Total attributed income amount received by the taxpayer under the
transferor trust measures. The transferor trust measures may apply if,
at any time, the taxpayer directly or indirectly transferred or caused
the transfer of property (including money) or services to a non-resident
trust estate. A trust is a non-resident trust estate if no trustee of
the trust estate was an Australian resident, and the trust estate’s
central management and control was not in Australia, at any time during
the income year.   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to trust income from a non-resident trust estate.
   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Work out total attributed income amount received by the taxpayer under
the transferor trust measures.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1996-97, this variable refers to trust income from a non-resident
trust estate.   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1996-97, work out total attributed income amount received by the
taxpayer under the transferor trust measures.
