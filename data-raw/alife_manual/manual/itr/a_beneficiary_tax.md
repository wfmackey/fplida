### a\_beneficiary\_tax

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Adjustments  
<br>**Question**:  
Amount on which ultimate beneficiary non-disclosure tax was payable  
<br>**Label**:  
Trust distribution on which beneficiary non-disclosure tax was payable  
<br>**General Notes**:  
The trust distributions amount you receive in the income year on which
the trustee paid ultimate beneficiary non-disclosure tax, less
deductible expenses. Amount used in the calculation of adjusted taxable
income.  
  
Ultimate beneficiary non-disclosure tax is payable by the trustee of a
closely held trust where net income of the trust is distributed to
another trust and the trustee does not, or cannot, disclose to the Tax
Office the name and certain other details about the person or persons
who are ultimately entitled to that income.  

  

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
Latest year 2004-05: this variable refers to the trust distributions
amount you received in the income year on which the trustee paid
ultimate beneficiary non-disclosure tax, less deductible expenses.  
  
Any trust distribution you received on which the trustee was liable for
ultimate beneficiary non-disclosure tax is not included in your
assessable income, and you do not pay income tax, Medicare levy or
Medicare levy surcharge on it. However, the distribution is taken into
account in determining your liability for superannuation contributions
surcharge.  
  
Any distribution that you show can be reduced by expenses you would have
been able to claim as a deduction if the distribution had been included
in your assessable income.   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Latest year 2004-05: add all trust distributions you received where
trustee was liable for beneficiary non-disclosure tax less expenses that
could have been claimed as a deduction if trust distribution was part of
your assessable income.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:

**2000**: Major; Introduced; New variable.

**2005**: Major; Discontinued; The Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 was
amended in 2007 and consequently trustees of closely held trusts were no
longer required to report the Commissioner of Taxation details of the
trust’s ultimate beneficiaries.

**2006**: Major; Discontinued; Variable discontinued.
