### sp\_status\_reported

<br>**Form**:  
ATO calculated  
<br>**Section**:  
NA  
<br>**Question**:  
NA  
<br>**Label**:  
DERIVED: Indicator whether client had spouse for full year, or whether
they partnered, unpartnered or both.   
<br>**General Notes**:  
Spousal status for the income year derived using labels L, M and N from
the Spouse details section in the individual income tax return form.  
  
*Code Definition*  
**0:** No information.  
**1:** Full year.  
**2:** Partnered.  
**3:** Unpartnered.  
**4:** Partnered/Unpartnered.
