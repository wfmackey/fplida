### it\_rept\_empl\_super\_cont

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Income tests  
<br>**Question**:  
Reportable employer superannuation contributions  
<br>**Label**:  
Reportable employer superannuation contributions  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Total amount of reportable super contributions made by the employer.  

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to reportable employer superannuation contributions
made by the employer on the taxpayer’s behalf. They are not included in
employee’s assessable income and are additional to the compulsory
contributions the employer must make.  
  
The following employer super contributions are reportable:  
- additional contributions as part of an employee’s individual salary
package  
- additional contributions under a salary sacrifice arrangement  
- pre-tax amounts paid to an employee’s super fund at the employee’s
direction, such as directing an annual bonus into super.   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Add up all reportable employer superannuation contributions amount shown
on payment summaries.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 2009-10, this variable refers to reportable employer superannuation
contributions made by the employer on the taxpayer’s behalf. They are
not included in employee’s assessable income and are additional to the
compulsory contributions the employer must make.

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:
