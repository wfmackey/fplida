### is\_psi\_net

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Income  
<br>**Question**:  
Personal service income (PSI): Net PSI - transferred from A item P1 on
page 12  
<br>**Label**:  
Net personal service income (PSI) as a sole trader  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Net amount of personal services income for personal services provided as
a sole trader. Those that earn PSI not as an employee may be unable to
claim certain deductions relating to their PSI. This depends on whether:
  - they have a personal services business determination from the
Commissioner of Taxation stating that your PSI was from conducting a
personal services business for the whole of the period they earned PSI,
or  
- satisfied one of the four tests outlined in Part A of the Business and
professional items section in Taxpack instructions.  
If they meet any of the above, they do not report PSI at this question.
   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to the net PSI earned as a sole trader and as
calculated in Business and professional items section A item P1:
Personal service income (PSI). Personal service income is income that is
mainly rewarded for an individual’s personal efforts or skills. Income
can include:  
- personal services income under a pay as you go (PAYG) voluntary
agreement  
- personal services income from which an amount was withheld because the
Australian business number (ABN) was not quoted  
- personal services income from working under a labour hire arrangement
as an independent contractor, payments (called specified payments) for
tutorial services provided to the Indigenous Student Success Programme
(formerly known as the Indigenous Tutorial Assistance Scheme) of the
Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet, translation and
interpretation services provided to the Translating and Interpreting
Service National of the Department of Home Affairs, or performing as a
performing artist in a promotional activity, etc.  
It does not include income:  
- for supplying or selling goods, for example, from retailing,
wholesaling or manufacturing  
- generated by an income-producing asset, for example, from operating a
bulldozer  
- earned for granting a right to use property, for example, the
copyright to a computer program  
- generated by a business structure, for example, a large accounting
firm.  
  
Special tax treatment may apply to certain PSI as they are subject to
special rules. This means that they are not able to claim certain
deductions, for example rent, mortgage interest, etc, unless they
satisfied the results test described in Business and professional items
section (see, for example, Taxpack 2017) and received a personal
services business determination(s) that was in force for the whole
period of earning PSI. If they meet the above requirements, they must
report their income under Business and professional items section, for
example, in tax form 2017-18, it is item P8: Business income and
expenses, which is then transferred to Supplementary section C item 15:
Net income or loss from business (is\_bus\_npp).  
   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Transfer the amount shown at A item P1 in the Business and professional
item section to is\_psi\_net. This amount is eligible PSI income less
deductions for payments to associates for principal work and total
amount of other deductions against PSI.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 2000-01, this variable refers to net personal services income earned
as a sole trader including:  
- personal services income under a Pay As You Go (PAYG) voluntary
agreement  
- personal services income from which an amount was withheld because
Australian Business Number (ABN) was not quoted  
- personal services income as an independent contractor under a labour
hire arrangement  
- personal services income from specified payments including payment for
tutorial services provided for the Aboriginal Tutorial Assistance Scheme
of the Department of Education, Training and Youth Affairs and payment
for translation and interpretation services for the Translating and
Interpreting Service of the Department of Immigration and Multicultural
Affairs  
- other personal services income.  
This does not include income:  
- for the sale or supply of goods or granting a right to use property
or  
- for the use of an asset.  
  
Special tax treatment may apply to certain PSI as they are subject to
special rules. This means that they are not able to claim certain
deductions, for example rent, mortgage interest, etc, unless they have a
prescribed payments system payee declaration that was in force and
received by the Commissioner as at 13 April 2000, and/or they received
80% or more of their PSI from one source and have a personal services
business determination(s) that was in force for the whole of the period.
If they meet the above requirements, they must report their income under
Business and professional items section item P8: Business income and
expenses, which is then transferred to Supplementary section C item 14:
Net income or loss from business (is\_bus\_npp).  
   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 2000-01, transfer the amount shown at M item P1 in the Business and
professional item section. This amount is eligible PSI income less
deductions for payments to associates for principal work and total
amount of other deductions against PSI.

<br>**Event time line: Significance, Type of change(s), Description of
change(s)**:  

**2001**: Major; Introduced; New variable.

**2002**: Minor; Component; Taxpayer does not report PSI under this
variable if:   - they have a prescribed payments system payee
declaration that was in force and received by the Commissioner as at 13
April 2000  
- they satisfied the results test  
- they received a personal services business determination that was in
force for the whole of the period they earned PSI.  
  

**2003**: Minor; Component; Does not include income generated by a
business structure for example, a large account firm.

**2005**: Minor; Component; Do not show personal services income
received as an employee or income that was subject to foreign resident
withholding. This is reported at item 14 Net income or loss from
business.
