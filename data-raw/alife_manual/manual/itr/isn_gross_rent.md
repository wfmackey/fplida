### isn\_gross\_rent

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Income  
<br>**Question**:  
Rent: Gross rent  
<br>**Label**:  
Gross rental income  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Supplementary income amount earned by the taxpayer that is derived from
Australian rental properties.  
  
The following amounts are also included as assessable income: bond money
retained in place of rent or damage to the property, insurance payouts
for lost rent and reimbursement of rental expenses claimed in an earlier
year.  
  
This amount excludes foreign source rental income.   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to the gross rental income, this must include any
bond money retained in place of rent or kept because of damage to the
property requiring repairs. An insurance payout for lost rent or a
reimbursement of any rental expenses claimed in an earlier year. This
amount excludes foreign source rental income, that is, rental income
from properties located outside Australia, this amount is reported at
Question: Foreign source income and foreign assets and property (for
example, in tax form 2017-18, see question 20).   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Calculate the gross income earned from renting out a property or a room
under a sharing economy.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1990-91, this variable refers to gross rental income, including
premiums. Lease premiums taxable as a capital gain should only be shown
at Question 14: Net capital gain. Rent from foreign source is shown at
Question 16: Attributed foreign income.   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1990-91, calculate the gross rent income from renting out a property
or a room under a sharing economy.
