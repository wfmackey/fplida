### a\_other\_inc

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Adjustments  
<br>**Question**:  
Government super contributions: Other income from employment or
business  
<br>**Label**:  
Income from other employment, business or partnership for government
super contributions adjustments  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Other income earned from employment, businesses, and partnership not
already reported, for example in tax form 2017-18, at Labels 1, 2, 3, 4,
12B or 15. Appicable to taxpayers who are eligible for super
co-contribution (which was introduced in July 2003) or low income super
contribution (which was introduced in July 2012) or low income super tax
offset (which was introduced in July 2017).   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to other income earned from employment, businesses,
and partnership. Applicable to taxpayers who are eligible for super
co-contribution, low income super contribution or low income super tax
offset.   
Taxpayer is eligible for:  
Super co-contribution, for example for 2017-18, if taxpayer has  
- made an eligible personal super contribution (not including amounts
claimed as deduction)  
- did not exceed your non-concessional contribution cap. - total
superannuation balance at 30 June 2017 was less than $1,600,000. - under
71 years old on 30 June 2018  
- taxable income less than $51,813, and   - at least some income from
employment or business (including partnership) - did not hold a
temporary visa at any time during 2017-18 (unless it is a New Zealand
citizenship or it was a prescribed visa)..  
  
Or Low income super contribution (or low income super tax offset ), for
example for 2017-18, if taxpayer has  
- adjusted taxable income (ATI) less than or equal to $37,000  
- concessional contributions made to complying super fund, and  
- 10% or more of total income from employment and/or business income
(including partnership),  
and received income from a partnership shown at item 13 of tax return.  
  
To complete question, taxpayer either  
- showed employment income or business income other than at the
following items: Labels 1 (Salary or wages), 2 (Allowances, earnings,
tips, directors fees etc), 3 (Employer lump sum payments), 4 (Employment
termination payments), 12B (Employee share scheme total assessable
discount amount) or IT1 (Total reportable fringe benefits amounts) and
IT2 (Reportable employer superannuation contributions) or in Business
and Professional Items form P1 (Personal services income) and P8
(Business income and expenses), or  
- received share of income from a partnerships (ignoring any losses
attributed), or   - income other than employment or business income,
or  
- employment or business income that related to another income year,
at  
- either Labels 1 (Salary or wages), 2 (Allowances, earnings, tips,
directors fees etc), 3 (Employer lump sum payments), 4 (Employment
termination payments), 12B (Employee share scheme total assessable
discount amount) or IT1 (Total reportable fringe benefits amounts) and
IT2 (Reportable employer superannuation contributions) or in Business
and Professional Items form P1 (Personal services income) and P8
(Business income and expenses).  
   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
(z): Sum of:  
- Employment or business income other than those at: Labels 1 (Salary or
wages), 2 (Allowances, earnings, tips, directors fees etc), 3 (Employer
lump sum payments), 4 (Employment termination payments), 12B (Employee
share scheme total assessable discount amount) or IT1 (Total reportable
fringe benefits amounts) and IT2 (Reportable employer superannuation
contributions) or in Business and Professional Items form P1 (Personal
services income) and P8 (Business income and expenses).  
  
(aa): Sum of:  
- Income from partnership (ignoring losses).  
  
(bb): Add (z) and (aa).  
  
(cc): Sum of:  
- Income other than employment or business income, or employment or
business income related to another income year in the following items:
Labels 1 (Salary or wages), 2 (Allowances, earnings, tips, directors
fees etc), 3 (Employer lump sum payments), 4 (Employment termination
payments), 12B (Employee share scheme total assessable discount amount)
or IT1 (Total reportable fringe benefits amounts) and IT2 (Reportable
employer superannuation contributions) or in Business and Professional
Items form P1 (Personal services income) and P8 (Business income and
expenses).  
  
(dd): Take (cc) away from (bb).  
  
(ee) Transfer amount at (dd) to a\_other\_inc.  
   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 2009-10, this variable refers to other income earned from
employment, businesses, and partnership for taxpayers who have made
eligible personal super contribution.   To claim, taxpayer must:  
- have made an eligible personal super contribution (for which you are
not claiming a deduction) to a complying super fund or retirement
savings account  
- be under 71 years old on 30 June 2010, and  
- have taxable income for 2009–10 less than $61,920.  
  
To complete question, taxpayer either:  
- showed employment income or business income other than at the
following items: Labels 1 (Salary or wages), 2 (Allowances, earnings,
tips, directors fees etc), 3 (Employer lump sum payments), 4 (Employment
termination payments), 12B (Employee share scheme total assessable
discount amount) or IT1 (Total reportable fringe benefits amounts) and
IT2 (Reportable employer superannuation contributions) or in Business
and Professional Items form P1 (Personal services income) and P8
(Business income and expenses), or  
- received share of income from a partnerships (ignoring any losses
attributed), or   - income other than employment or business income,
shown at  
- either Labels 1 (Salary or wages), 2 (Allowances, earnings, tips,
directors fees etc), 3 (Employer lump sum payments), 4 (Employment
termination payments), 12B (Employee share scheme total assessable
discount amount) or IT1 (Total reportable fringe benefits amounts) and
IT2 (Reportable employer superannuation contributions) or in Business
and Professional Items form P1 (Personal services income) and P8
(Business income and expenses).
