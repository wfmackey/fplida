### onra\_sole\_parent

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Rebates/tax offsets  
<br>**Question**:  
Sole parent  
<br>**Label**:  
Sole parent rebate amount available to claim.   
<br>**General Notes**:  
Sole parent rebate amount available to claim. This offset was abolished
in 2000.   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
Latest year: 1999-2000. This variable refers to the amount of rebate the
taxpayer is entitled to claim under the sole parent rebate.   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Latest year: 1999-2000. Write the amount of sole parent rebate entitled
to claim.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1990-91, this variable refers to the sole parent rebate the taxpayer
is entitled to claim if the taxpayer is a sole carer of a dependent
child or student for the whole or part of the financial year. They were
solely responsible for looking after, bringing up, and maintaining a
dependent child or student. The maximum sole parent rebate available to
claim is $1,015.   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1990-91,   (a) Calculate number of days of sole care.  
(b) Divide (a) by 365.  
(c) Multiply (b) by $1,015. This is the rebate that can be claimed under
onra\_sole\_parent.
