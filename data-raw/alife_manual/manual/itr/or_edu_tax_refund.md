### or\_edu\_tax\_refund

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Tax offsets  
<br>**Question**:  
Education tax refund  
<br>**Label**:  
Education tax refund - refundable tax offset claimed.   
<br>**General Notes**:  
Education tax refund offset amount the taxpayer is eligible to claim for
education expenses for the primary and secondary school studies of a
child or for education expenses of the taxpayer if the taxpayer is an
independent student under 25 years old.   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
Latest year: 2010-11. This variable refers to the education tax refund
(ETR) offset amount the taxpayer is eligible to claim for education
expenses for the primary or secondary school studies of a child or for
education expenses of the taxpayer if the taxpayer is an independent
student under 25 years old. To be eligible to claim the ETR, the
taxpayer must have met one of the four conditions and the student must
have met the school requirement.  
  
Condition 1: Taxpayer or partner received or eligible to receive Family
Tax Benefit (FTB) Part A for their child.  
Condition 2: Taxpayer or partner received payments other than FTB for
that child. This includes a social security pension or benefit, a Labour
Market Program payment, or a prescribed educational scheme payment.  
Condition 3: The child was 16 years or older, was not attending primary
or secondary school studies and did not earn any income during the
income year.  
Condition 4: Taxpayer is an independent student if they were under 25
years old and were receiving a social security pension or benefit, a
Labour Market Program payment or a prescribed educational scheme
payment, they met the independence requirements for the payment, they
were an Australian resident (under the Social Security Act 1991) or a
special category visa holder (under the Migration Act 1958), were
residing in Australia, and no one else, such as a parent or an approved
care organisation, was entitled to the ETR on their behalf.  
  
(See TaxPack 2011 for more details).    

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Latest year: 2010-11. Calculate the amount of eligible education
expenses that can be claimed.
