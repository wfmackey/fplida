### ml\_depend\_child

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Medicare levy related items  
<br>**Question**:  
Medicare levy reduction or exemption: Reduction based on family income -
Number of dependent children and students.  
<br>**Label**:  
Number of dependent children or students used for calculating Medicare
levy reduction.  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Number of dependent children or students reported by the taxpayer for
the purposes of calculating the Medicare levy reduction. Pre-2003, the
taxpayer also records number of dependent children or students if they
are eligible for the half or full levy exemption.   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to the number of dependent children used to
calculate the family taxable income limit, which is used to determine
whether or not the taxpayer is eligible for the Medicare Levy reduction.
Australian residents for tax purposes are subject to a Medicare levy
(for example, in 2017-18: it is 2.0% of their taxable income) unless
they qualify for a reduction or exemption.  
  
A dependent child is any child who was an Australian resident whom the
taxpayer maintained in the income year and whose adjusted taxable income
was less than the ATI thresholds. A dependent child can be defined as
either any full-time student aged under 25 years old at a school,
college or university, or any child under 21 years old and was not a
full time student.  
  
If the taxpayer had a spouse on 30 June of that income year or their
spouse died during the income year, they must include all dependent
children. If the taxpayer was single or separated on 30 June of that
income year, they can only count the number of dependent children for
whom they received the family tax benefit during all or part of the
income year.   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Write the number of dependent children based on the above criteria.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1990-91, this variable refers to the number of dependent children
and students, which is used to determine whether the taxpayer is
eligible for a Medicare levy reduction. Australian residents for tax
purposes are subject to a Medicare levy of 1.25% of their taxable income
above $12,528 and 20 cents for every dollar between $11,745 and $12,528.
This question only applies to a taxpayer who had income greater than the
threshold of $11,745 during the income year and either:  
(a) Had a spouse (married or de facto) on 30 June 1991 and the combined
taxable income was less than $20,316 plus $2,240 for each dependent
child or student.   (b) Was entitled to a rebate as a sole parent or for
a housekeeper or daughter-housekeeper and the taxable income was less
than $20,316 plus $2,240 for each dependent child or student.  
   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1990-91, if the taxpayer had income greater than the threshold of
$11,745 during the income year and either:  
(a) Had a spouse (married or de facto) on 30 June 1991 and the combined
taxable income was less than $20,316 plus $2,240 for each dependent
child or student.   (b) Was entitled to a rebate as a sole parent or for
a housekeeper or daughter-housekeeper and the taxable income is less
than $20,316 plus $2,240 for each dependent child or student.  
Write the number of dependent children and students.

<br>**Event time line: Significance, Type of change(s), Description of
change(s)**:  

**1992**: Minor; Component; Group (a) also includes if the spouse died
during the year ending 30 June 1992.

**1994**: Moderate; Question; The basic Medicare levy increased to 1.4%
of taxable income above a certain threshold.

**1996**: Major; Component; Change in requirements on whether taxpayer
is eligible for full exemption, half exemption, or reductions based on
income (see TaxPack 1996 Deductions and rebate sections for more
details). If the taxpayer meets any of the above, they record the number
of dependent children and students at this variable.

The basic Medicare levy increased to 1.5% of taxable income above a
certain threshold.

**1997**: Moderate; Component; The basic Medicare levy increased to 1.7%
of taxable income.

**1998**: Moderate; Component; The basic Medicare levy decreased to 1.5%
of taxable income.

**1999**: Minor; Component; Dependent children and students refers to:  
- any child under 16 the taxpayer maintained and was not a full time
student and whose separate net income was less than a certain threshold
or   - any full-time student under 25 whose separate net income was less
than a certain threshold. (See TaxPack 1999 Deductions and rebate
sections for more details).  
If the taxpayer was not married on 30 June 1999 or was married but
living separately from their spouse, for any child or student to be
included within the term ‘dependent children and students’, family
allowance must have been payable to them in respect of that child or
student for the whole or part of the financial year.  
  

**2003**: Major; Component; Taxpayer only records the number of
dependent children and students if they are eligible for the Medicare
levy reduction based on family income. Not required to record number of
dependent children student if they meet half or full levy exemption.

**2015**: Moderate; Component; Medicare levy increased to 2.0% of
taxable income above a certain threshold.
