### is\_fsi\_rent

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Income  
<br>**Question**:  
Foreign source income and foreign assets or property: Net foreign rent  
<br>**Label**:  
Net foreign rent income  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Foreign rental income including foreign tax that was taken out less
deductible expenses that were incurred in earning the foreign rental
income, excluding any debt deductions.  
  
Incurred is defined as 1) received a bill or invoice for an expense that
you are liable for and must pay (even if you don’t pay it until after
the end of the income year) or 2) do not receive a bill or invoice but
you are charged and you pay for the expense.  
  
Debt deductions, such as interest and borrowing costs, are not
deductible for the purposes of this calculation unless they are related
to income earned through a permanent establishment in an overseas
country.  
  

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to foreign rental income including foreign tax that
was taken out less deductible expenses that were incurred in earning the
foreign rental income, excluding any debt deductions.   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
(a) Add total rental income, including any foreign tax that was taken
out.  
(b) Add up all the deductible expenses incurred in earning foreign
rental income, excluding debt deductions.  
(c) Take the amount at (b) away from (a) and transfer to
is\_fsi\_rent.  
   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 2008-09, this variable refers to net foreign rental income received
including the foreign tax that was taken out less deductible expenses.  

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 2008-09,    
(a) Add total rental income, including any foreign tax that was taken
out.  
(b) Add up all the deductible expenses incurred in earning foreign
rental income, excluding debt deductions.  
(c) Take the amount at (b) away from (a) and transfer to
is\_fsi\_rent.
