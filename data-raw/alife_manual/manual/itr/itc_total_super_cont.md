<br>**Label**:  
ATO: Total reportable superannuation contributions for Medicare levy
surcharge purposes.   
<br>**General Notes**:  
Total reportable superannuation contributions amount calculated by the
ATO to determine Medicare Levy Surcharge liability. Data available from
2010 income year due to change in ATO systems.   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
Total reportable superannuation contributions for Medicare levy
surcharge purposes is the sum of:  
- reportable employer super contributions  
- deductible personal super contributions   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Add up reportable employer super contributions and deductible personal
super contributions.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 2009-2010: Total reportable superannuation contributions for
Medicare levy surcharge purposes is the sum of:  
- reportable employer super contributions  
- deductible personal super contributions   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 2009-2010: Add up reportable employer super contributions and
deductible personal super contributions.
