The Australian Taxation Office (ATO) has a registry of client details.
This includes individual who have interacted with the ATO for either
taxation or superannuation matters. The client registry contains the
gender, date of birth and date of death variables. Each variable
contains a spectrum of credibility ratings based on the source of the
data and the verifications performed on the data. For the purpose of
ALife, the variables have been selected in order of credibility, with
additional tests to further validate the data.
