### tw\_equalisation\_tax

<br>**Section**:

<br>**Question**:  
Net income equalisation and/or farm management deposits or withdrawals:
Tax withheld from income equalisation scheme on farm management deposits
or withdrawals.   
<br>**Label**:  
Tax withheld from income equalisation scheme on farm management deposits
or withdrawals.   
<br>**General Notes**:  
Tax withheld from income equalisation scheme on farm management deposits
or withdrawals.   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
Latest year: 1998-99. This variable refers to the tax amount withheld
from income equalisation scheme on farm management deposits or
withdrawals.   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Latest year: 1998-99. Write the amount of tax withheld from income
equalisation scheme on farm management deposits or withdrawals.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1990-91, this variable refers to tax withheld from income
equalisation scheme on farm management deposits or withdrawals. Deposits
are tax deductible in the year of deposit and assessable as income in
the year in which the request for withdrawal is made.   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1990-91, write the amount of tax withheld from income equalisation
scheme on farm management deposits or withdrawals.
