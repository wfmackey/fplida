### on\_child\_secondary

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Tax offsets  
<br>**Question**:  
Education tax refund  
<br>**Label**:  
Number of secondary school students  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Taxpayers’ number of secondary school children. Information reported to
determine eligibility to claim the education tax refund. Information not
available after the education tax refund was discontinued in 2012.   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
Latest year: 2010-11. This variable refers to the number of secondary
school children that the taxpayer is claiming the education tax refund
(ETR) for if they incurred eligible education expenses.   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Latest year: 2010-11. Add up the number of secondary school students
that you are claiming ETR for.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 2008-09, this variable refers to the number of secondary school
children that the taxpayer is claiming the education tax refund (ETR)
for if they incurred eligible education expenses.   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 2008-09, add up the number of secondary school students that you are
claiming ETR for.
