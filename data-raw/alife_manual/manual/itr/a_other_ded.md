### a\_other\_ded

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Adjustments  
<br>**Question**:  
Government super contributions: Other deductions from business income  
<br>**Label**:  
Other deductions from business income for government super contributions
adjustments  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Other business deductions for taxpayers who are eligible for super
co-contribution (which was introduced in July 2003) or low income super
contribution (which was introduced in July 2012) or low income super tax
offset (which was introduced in July 2017). Includes business deductions
other than those reported in Business and Professional Items from P8
(Business income and expenses). Examples include partnership loss
calculated in labour hire payments, personal services income deductions
or other deductions against business income.   

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable refers to other business deductions for taxpayers who are
eligible for super co-contribution, low income super contribution, or
low income super tax offset.  
To claim, taxpayer must be eligible for:  
Super co-contribution, i.e. , for example for 2017-18, if taxpayer has  
- made an eligible personal super contribution (for which you are not
claiming a deduction)  
- did not exceed your non-concessional contribution cap  
- total superannuation balance at 30 June 2017 was less than
$1,600,000  
- under 71 years old on 30 June 2018  
- taxable income less than $51,813, and   - at least some income from
employment or business (including partnership)  
- did not hold a temporary visa at any time during 2017-18 (unless it is
a New Zealand citizenship or it was a prescribed visa).  
  
Or low income super contribution or low income super tax offset, i.e.,
for example for 2017-18, if taxpayer has:  
- adjusted taxable income (ATI) less than or equal to $37,000  
- concessional contributions made to complying super fund, and  
- 10% or more of total income from employment and/or business income
(including partnership).  
And received income from a partnership shown at Partnership and Trusts
in supplementary tax return form.  
  
Furthermore, taxpayer has  
- had business deductions in the income year other than those included
in the deductions items at item P8 in the Business and professional
items schedule. Examples include: share of partnership loss, deductions
relating to partnership distribution, personal service income
deductions, or deductions related to managing your business tax
affairs.  
   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Total amount of business deductions other than those reported in
Business and Professional Items from P8.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 2009-10, this variable refers to other business deductions for
taxpayers who have made an eligible personal super contribution. / To
claim, taxpayer must  
- have made an eligible personal super contribution (for which you are
not claiming a deduction) to a complying super fund or retirement
savings account  
- be under 71 years old on 30 June 2010, and  
- have taxable income for 2009–10 less than $61,920.  
  
Furthermore, taxpayer has  
- had business deductions in 2009-10 other than those included in the
deductions items at item P8 in the Business and professional items
schedule 2009-10.  
  
Examples include:  
- the business portion of a distributed partnership loss included in
calculating the amount at N or O at item 13 (as long as the partnership
carries on a business)  
- deductions at X or Y at item 13 which relate to the business income
portion of a partnership distribution  
- personal service income deductions at item P1 on the Business and
professional item schedule for individuals 2009-10  
- deductions shown at D10 for costs involved in managing your business
tax affairs as a sole trader or partnership business.  
   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 2009-10, total amount of business deductions other than those
reported in Business and Professional Items from P8.
