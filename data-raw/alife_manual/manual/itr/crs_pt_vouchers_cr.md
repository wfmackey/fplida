### crs\_pt\_vouchers\_cr

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Partnerships and trusts  
<br>**Question**:  
Income tax credit vouchers relating to partnership or trust
distributions  
<br>**Label**:  
Tax on partnership or trust distributions by purchasing tax stamps or
income tax credit vouchers  
<br>**General Notes**:  
If taxpayer voluntarily paid tax on partnership and trust distributions
by purchasing tax stamps or income tax credit vouchers during the income
year, the amount of tax paid is recorded under crs\_pt\_vouchers\_cr.  

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
Latest year: 1998-1999. This variable relates to the amount of income
tax credit vouchers purchased for the taxpayer to offset tax on net
income from a partnership or trust included in the amounts shown at:   
- label N: Distribution from primary production partnerships
(pt\_is\_pship\_dist\_pp)  
- label L: Distribution from primary production trusts
(pt\_is\_trust\_dist\_pp)  
- label O: distributions from non-primary production partnerships less
foreign income (pt\_is\_pship\_dist\_npp), and  
- label U: Distribution from non-primary production trusts less net
capital gains and foreign income (pt\_is\_trust\_dist\_npp)   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Latest year: 1998-99. Total amount of income tax vouchers.   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1995-96, if taxpayer received income in a partnership or as a
beneficiary of a trust (is\_pt\_net\_dist\_npp or is\_pt\_net\_dist\_pp)
and voluntarily paid tax on partnership and trust distributions by
purchasing tax stamps or income tax credit vouchers during the income
year, the amount of tax paid is recorded under crs\_pt\_vouchers\_cr.  

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1995-96, tax paid by taxpayer for any tax stamps or income tax
credit vouchers purchased during 1994-95.
