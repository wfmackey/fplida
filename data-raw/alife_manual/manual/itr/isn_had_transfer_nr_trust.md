### isn\_had\_transfer\_nr\_trust

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Income  
<br>**Question**:  
Foreign entities: Have you ever, either directly or indirectly, caused
the transfer of property, including money, or services to a non-resident
trust estate?  
<br>**Label**:  
Binary indicator of whether taxpayer had interest in transferor trusts  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Binary indicator for whether the taxpayer, given they are an Australian
resident for tax purposes, had, at any time, directly or indirectly
transferred or caused the transfer of property (including money) or
services to a non-resident trust.  

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable is a binary indicator which describes whether taxpayer,
who is an Australian residents for tax purpose, had, at any time,
directly or indirectly transferred or caused the transfer of property
(including money) or services to a non-resident trust. A trust is a
non-resident trust if no trustee of the trust estate was an Australian
resident, and the trust estate’s central management and control was not
in Australia, at any time during the income year.   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Did you, at any time, directly or indirectly transfer or cause the
transfer of property (including money) or services to a non-resident
trust?  
- No: Print X in the ‘No’ box (at W item 19 in tax form 2017-18).  
- Yes: Print X in the ‘Yes’ box (at W item 19 in tax form 2017-18).  
   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1996-97, this variable is a binary indicator which describes if
taxpayer had at any time transferred property, including money, or
services to a trust which, during the income year, was a non-resident
trust.   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1996-97,   Did you, at any time, directly or indirectly cause the
transfer of property, including money, or services to a non-resident
trust?  
- No: Print N at answer box A.  
- Yes: Print Y at answer box A.

<br>**Event time line: Significance, Type of change(s), Description of
change(s)**:  

**1997**: Major; Introduced; New variable.

**2000**: Major; Question; Change in variable definition. Variable is
now a binary indicator which describes whether the taxpayer ever, either
directly or indirectly, caused the transfer of property, including
money, or services to a non resident trust estate.

Question changed from “Did you have an interest in a Foreign Investment
Fund (FIF) or a Foreign Life assurance Policy (FLP)?” to “Have you ever,
either directly or indirectly, cause the transfer of property, including
money, or services to a non resident trust estate?”
