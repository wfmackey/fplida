### isn\_cfc\_had\_interest

<br>**Form**:  
Individual Tax Return  
<br>**Section**:  
Supplementary section: Income  
<br>**Question**:  
Foreign entities: Did you have either a direct or indirect interest in a
controlled foreign company (CFC)?  
<br>**Label**:  
Binary indicator of whether taxpayer had either a direct or indirect
interest in a CFC  
<br>**General Notes**:  
Binary indicator for whether the taxpayer held a direct or indirect
interest in a controlled foreign company.  

<br>**Latest or Current Conditions**:  
This variable is a binary indicator which describes whether a taxpayer,
who is an Australian resident for tax purpose, had either a direct or an
indirect interest in a controlled foreign company (CFC) such that they
alone, or with associates, had direct or indirect controlling interests
totalling 10% or more in a foreign company or have effective control of
a foreign company. Temporary residents do not need to show foreign
investment income from CFC.  
  
*Code Definition*  
**Y:** Taxpayer is an Australian resident for tax purposes and have
direct or indirect controlling interests totalling 10% or more in a
foreign company, or have effective control of a foreign company.  
**N:**Taxpayer did not have a direct or indirect interest in a
controlled foreign company (CFC) or if they did, they did not meet the
requirement of Y category.  
   

<br>**Latest or Current Calculations**:  
Did you have either a direct or indirect interest in a CFC?  
No: Print N at I item 19.   Yes: Read on.  
Did you, alone or with associates:  
- have direct or indirect controlling interests totalling 10% or more in
a foreign company, or  
- have effective control of a foreign company?  
No: Print N at I item 19.  
Yes: Print Y at I item 19.  
   

<br>**Earliest Conditions**:  
For 1996-97, this variable is a binary indicator which describes if the
taxpayer is an Australian resident with interest in a controlled foreign
company (CFC) or non-resident trust. Taxpayer has interest in a CFC if
they, alone or with associates, either have direct or indirect control
interests totalling 10 per cent or more in a foreign company or have
effective control of a foreign company.  
  
*Code Definition*  
**Y:** Taxpayer is an Australian resident for tax purposes and had
either a direct or an indirect interest in a controlled foreign company
(CFC) or non-resident trust.  
**N:** Taxpayer did not have a direct or indirect interest in a
controlled foreign company (CFC) or if they did, they did not meet the
requirement of Y category.  
   

<br>**Earliest Calculations**:  
For 1996-97,  
Step 1: Did you have an interest in a non-resident trust?  
- Yes: Print Y at answer box I.  
- No: Read on.  

Step 2: Do you have a direct or an indirect control interest of 10% or
more in a foreign company or trust? OR Do you (alone or with associates)
have effective control of a foreign company?  
- No: Print N at answer box I.  
- Yes: Print Y at answer box I.
