<br>**General Notes**:  
This is the Division 293 taxable super contribution from contributions
of accumulation accounts.  
  
Division 293 tax, started in income year 2013, is an additional tax on
super contributions which reduces the tax concession for individuals
whose combined income and super contributions are greater than the
Division 293 threshold.  
  
From 1 July 2017, the threshold at which a Division 293 tax calculation
will result in an assessment being issued is $250,000. For 1 July 2012
to 30 June 2017, the threshold is $300,000.  
  
Division 293 tax is payable on the excess over the threshold, or on the
Division 293 super contributions, whichever is less. The rate of
Division 293 tax is 15%.
