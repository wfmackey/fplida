### sb\_mem\_bal

<br>**Form**:  
Super member contributions statement  
<br>**Section**:  
Member account balance  
<br>**Question**:  
Account balance  
<br>**Label**:  
Balance of all the member’s super accounts  
<br>**General Notes**:  
This variable refers to the total closing balance of the member’s
accounts on 30 June. For account-based accumulation interest, this
amount is determined according to ordinary account principles before
adjustment. For defined benefit and other non-account based interest,
the amount recorded should be equivalent to any amount reported in
periodic statement issued to the member.
